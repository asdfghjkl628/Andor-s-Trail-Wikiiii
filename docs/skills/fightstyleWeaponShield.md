## Effect

Gives benefits when fighting with a weapon in the main hand and having a shield or parrying weapon equipped in the off-hand.



Every skill level increases the attack chance of weapons in the main hand with 25 % of their original attack chances, and the block chance of shields or parrying weapons by 25 % of their original block chances.

## Requirements per skill level

| Skill level | Character level |
|---|---|
| 1 | 15 |
| 2 | 30 |
