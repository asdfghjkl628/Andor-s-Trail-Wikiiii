    Ratdom_maze¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID ratdom_maze
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 25
    Started by stepping on a trigger on ratdom_maze1, stepping on a trigger on ratdom_maze2
    NPCs involved Wart
    Locations ratdom_maze_624
    Related quests 2

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on ratdom_maze1. Required:

    • wearing Ratcave Torch
