    It makes no fence¶
    Quest ID tunlon_fence
    In journal Yes
    Stages 16 (completes at 12, 250)
    Started by Tunlon (Bwmfill 3)
    NPCs involved Hadracor, Jakrar, Tinlyn, Tunlon, Villager, Wood craftsman
    Locations Brimhaven 2 woodcutter, Bwmfill 3, Fallhaven south-west, Fields 6
    Total XP 7,005
    Related quests 5

    Overview¶

    I found Tunlon, a sheep farmer, on the hill next to Crossglen. He asked me to help him get some wood for new fences. Since Crossglen doesn't have a lumberjack, I should go to Fallhaven and ask there.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Tunlon (Bwmfill 3). Required:

    • reached stage 250 of It makes no fence
    • NOT reached stage 10 of It makes no fence

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.

    • Needs: killed 1× Mountain Sheep; not reached stage 44 of Blackwater Mountain story flags (hidden flag)
    • “My poor sheep! What have you done?!”

    Stage 40 · Tunlon · 1 way

    • Needs: stage 10, 20, 32; reached stage 21 of Cheap cuts
    • “Please go and ask him, if he knows anyone who could help.”

    Stage 100 · Tinlyn · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Tinlyn, choose “Yes. And he made me ask you if you knew where you could get fences from.”

    Stage 110 · Tinlyn · 1 way

    Stage 210 · Villager · 1 way
