## Effect

Gives benefits when using weapons that require both hands to wield, such as two-handed swords, greataxes or giant hammers.



Every skill level increases damage potential of two-handed weapons with 30 % of the original damage potential.

## Requirements per skill level

| Skill level | Character level |
|---|---|
| 1 | 15 |
| 2 | 30 |
