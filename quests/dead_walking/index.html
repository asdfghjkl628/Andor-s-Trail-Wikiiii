    How each stage is reached¶

    Every dialogue route found in the game data, including alternatives that end up in the same place. "Conditions" are everything checked along that dialogue path.

    Stage 0: 1 route
    1. Talk to Gabriel (vilegard_s) → choose “Of course. Anything for the Shadow.” — conditions: NOT reached stage 0 of The Dead are Walking → stage 0. NPC: “Outstanding. Report back to me when you are done.”
    Stage 10: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on road2 → choose “What an eerie sound ...” — conditions: reached stage 0 of The Dead are Walking; NOT reached stage 40 of The Dead are Walking → stage 10. NPC: “Now you notice that the moaning heard by Gabriel is a little louder here.”
    Stage 20: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on road5 → choose “I should stick around a little bit longer.” — conditions: reached stage 0 of The Dead are Walking; NOT reached stage 20 of The Dead are Walking → stage 20. NPC: “Now you begin to notice that the moaning heard by Gabriel is a little louder here.”
    Stage 40: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on haunted_forest1 → the conversation leads here automatically — conditions: NOT reached stage 40 of The Dead are Walking → stage 40. NPC: “As you enter this dark place, you suspect that you are getting closer to the sounds heard by Gabriel as the moaning is…”
    Stage 50: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on haunted_house_basement → the conversation leads here automatically — conditions: NOT reached stage 50 of The Dead are Walking → stage 50; also applies condition fear. NPC: “Again, yelling in a language that you do not understand. You begin to tremble in fear.”
    Stage 60: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on haunted_house_basement → the conversation leads here automatically — conditions: killed 1× Benzimos; NOT reached stage 60 of The Dead are Walking → stage 60. NPC: “Benzimos is now dead ... again.”
