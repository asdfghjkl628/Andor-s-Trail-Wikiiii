    Search for Andor¶
    Quest ID andor
    In journal Yes
    Stages 37 (completes at 914)
    Started by Mikhail (Home)
    NPCs involved Alkapoan, Andor, Bryma, Buceth, Bucus, Elysa +18
    Locations Blackwater mountain 12, Brightport forest, Brightport school 7, Brightport thieves
    Total XP 7,000
    Related quests 26

    Version note

    From v0.7.0 to v0.8.13, this quest could not be completed: it had no ending yet. It became completable in v0.8.14.

    Overview¶

    My father Mikhail says that Andor has not been home since yesterday. I should go look for him in the village.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Mikhail (Home). Required:

    • reached stage 100 of Breakfast bread
    • reached stage 100 of Rats!

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
