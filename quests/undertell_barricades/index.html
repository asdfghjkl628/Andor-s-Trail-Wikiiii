    You shall pass¶
    Quest ID undertell_barricades
    In journal Yes
    Stages 14 (completes at 160)
    Started by Myrelis (Galmore 58)
    NPCs involved Bela, Bela, Benbyr, Drunkard, Myrelis, Shannal
    Locations Crossroads, Fallhaven north-west, Galmore 58, Mt galmore railhouse
    Total XP 7,204
    Related quests 4

    Overview¶

    I asked Myrelis how to get the barricades to Undertell removed and he told me to meet his friend Shannal inside the beach-scene house.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Myrelis (Galmore 58). Required:

    • reached stage 9 of Galmore story flags (hidden flag)
    • latest stage of Lost treasures is 35
    • NOT reached stage 20 of You shall pass

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
