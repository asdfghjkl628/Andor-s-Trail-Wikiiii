    A strange looking dagger¶
    Quest ID brv_dagger
    In journal Yes
    Stages 26 (completes at 70, 115, 120, 200, 230)
    Started by Edrin (brimhaven_metalsmith)
    NPCs involved Arlish, Edrin, Forlin, Ito, Kizzo, Ogea +3

    Total XP 3,000
    Related quests 2

    Overview¶

    I asked Edrin about the strange-looking dagger I purchased. He told me he recognizes it because he made it many years ago, and it used to have a gem mounted in the pommel. If I can find the gem he can remount it in the dagger, and the dagger will have special properties. Apparently it used to belong to someone named Lawellyn.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Edrin (brimhaven_metalsmith). Required:

    • carry 1× A strange looking dagger
    • NOT reached stage 70 of A strange looking dagger
    • NOT reached stage 50 of A strange looking dagger
    • NOT reached stage 25 of A strange looking dagger
    • NOT reached stage 80 of A strange looking dagger
    • NOT reached stage 20 of A strange looking dagger
    • NOT carry 1× A strange-looking gem
