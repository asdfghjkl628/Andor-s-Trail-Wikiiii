## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Arcir](../monsters/arcir.md). Required:

- hand over 1× [Dusty old book](../items/brv_wh_item_09.md)
- reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10)
- reached stage 20 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-20)

## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Arcir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Arcir](../monsters/arcir.md), choose “And I'm your delivery kid. Did you order a 'Dusty old book'?”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Dusty old book](../items/brv_wh_item_09.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 20 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-20)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 20 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-20), 20× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Yes, an old but useful book, but you should have wiped it off first. Anyway, here's my delivery fee.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Edrin · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Edrin](../monsters/brv_metalsmith.md), choose “Hello, did you order a 'Striped Hammer'?”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Striped hammer](../items/brv_wh_item_08.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 30 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-30)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 30 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-30), 10× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Ah, yes, a striped hammer. Don't look so impatient. Here's my delivery charge, and now you can leave!”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Odirath · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Odirath](../monsters/stoutford_armorer.md), choose “Did you really order such an ugly porcelain figure? Oops, sorry I didn't mean to offend you.”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Pretty porcelain figure](../items/brv_wh_item_07.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 40 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-40)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 40 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-40), 30× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Yes, indeed. A gift for my beautiful daughter... but why did it take so long? Sigh, here's my delivery fee.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Venanra · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Venanra](../monsters/brv_laundry_boss.md), choose “Did you order an 'Old, worn cape'?”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Old, worn cape](../items/brv_wh_item_06.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 50 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-50)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 50 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-50), 10× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Yes I did, kid. Incredibly, it has become the latest fashion to buy new capes with holes. Here's my delivery fee.”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Tjure · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tjure](../monsters/tjure.md), choose “Indeed. By the way, did you order a 'Mysterious green something'?”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 200 of [The silver scale](../quests/mermaid_scale.md#stage-200); hand over 1× [Mysterious green something](../items/brv_wh_item_05.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 60 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-60)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 60 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-60), 50× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“What?! My lucky clover...but why now? Anyway, I'll no longer run out of luck. Here's my delivery fee.”*


<span id="route-60"></span>

??? note "Stage 60 · Servant · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Servant](../monsters/erwyn_servant.md#v-guynmart_servant), choose “I'm here to give you your ordered item. You don't want it?”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Chandelier](../items/brv_wh_item_04.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 70 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-70)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 70 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-70), 40× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Finally, I'm no longer afraid of that room every time my lord turns off the lights to scare me. Here's my delivery fee.”*


<span id="route-70"></span>

??? note "Stage 70 · Arghes · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Arghes](../monsters/arghes.md), choose “And how interesting that you ordered a pair of 'Yellow boots'. Did you really order this?”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 51 of [Search for Andor](../quests/andor.md#stage-51); hand over 1× [Yellow boot](../items/brv_wh_item_03.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 80 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-80)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 80 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-80), 50× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Yes kid, thank you. Here, take this gold for them.”*


<span id="route-80"></span>

??? note "Stage 80 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “Yes, I came back to deliver your order of a 'Lyre'. You must be good at playing it?”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 90 of [Uncertain cause](../quests/wrye.md#stage-90); hand over 1× [Lyre](../items/brv_wh_item_02.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 90 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-90)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 90 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-90)
    - *“Yes, I'm longing for it just like how I'm longing for my son. But now I can mourn as I play his favorite song until I die.”*


<span id="route-90"></span>

??? note "Stage 90 · Mikhail · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Mikhail](../monsters/mikhail.md), choose “Yes, I'm here to deliver the order for a 'Plush Pillow'. But what for?”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 10 of [Breakfast bread](../quests/mikhail_bread.md#stage-10); hand over 1× [Plush pillow](../items/brv_wh_item_01.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 100 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-100)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 100 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-100)
    - *“Oh wow! Finally, your brother's gift has arrived and we only have to wait for his arrival.”*


<span id="route-100"></span>

??? note "Stage 100 · Pangitain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Pangitain](../monsters/brv_fortune_teller.md), choose “So you are the one who ordered a 'Crystal Globe'?”

    - **Needs:** hand over 1× [Crystal globe](../items/brv_wh_item_00.md); reached stage 10 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-10); reached stage 110 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-110)
    - **Gives:** clears stage 110 of [Delivery](../quests/brv_wh_delivery.md#stage-110), 100× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - *“Ah yes, I need a new one. My current crystal globe has become a bit cloudy - otherwise I would of course have seen that you would bring me…”*
