    Overview¶

    At the Wexlow well, I angered something evil and powerful (named Gamjee, I think) to the point that it pulled me down into the well. I've landed at the well's bottom severely injured, but alive.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on Wexlow village. Required:

    • NOT reached stage 10 of Echoes of enchantment
    • NOT reached stage 11 of Echoes of enchantment
    • NOT reached stage 13 of Echoes of enchantment
    • NOT reached stage 9 of Echoes of enchantment
    • hand over 1× Small rock
    • hand over 1× Leather boots
    • hand over 1× Rotten meat
    • hand over 1× Bone
