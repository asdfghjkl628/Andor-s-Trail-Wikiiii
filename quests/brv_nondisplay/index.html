    brv_nondisplay¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track progress. It does not appear in the journal. The stage descriptions below are internal notes written by the developers and may be brief.
    Quest ID brv_nondisplay
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 36
    Started by stepping on a trigger on brimhaven_inn_east
    NPCs involved Alkapoan, Churrie, Feygard soldier, Feygard soldier, Feygard soldier, Gnossath +5
    Locations brimhaven1, brimhaven4, brimhaven_fortune_teller, brimhaven_house1
    Total XP 500
    Related quests 7
