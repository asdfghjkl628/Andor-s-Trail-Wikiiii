## Overview

> I found Tunlon, a sheep farmer, on the hill next to Crossglen. He asked me to help him get some wood for new fences. Since Crossglen doesn't have a lumberjack, I should go to Fallhaven and ask there.

## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Tunlon](../monsters/tunlon.md) ([Bwmfill 3](../maps/bwmfill3.md)). Required:

- reached stage 250 of [It makes no fence](../quests/tunlon_fence.md#stage-250)
- NOT reached stage 10 of [It makes no fence](../quests/tunlon_fence.md#stage-10)

    **Way 1:** Stepping on a trigger on [Bwmfill 3](../maps/bwmfill3.md)

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 12, 250; killed 1× [Mountain Sheep](../monsters/bwm_sheep1.md)

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Tunlon](../monsters/tunlon.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 12, 250; killed 1× [Mountain Sheep](../monsters/bwm_sheep1.md)

    **Way 3:** Stepping on a trigger on [Bwmfill 3](../maps/bwmfill3.md)

    - **Needs:** killed 1× [Mountain Sheep](../monsters/bwm_sheep1.md); not reached stage 44 of [Blackwater Mountain story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/bwmfill_nondisplay.md#stage-44)
    - *“My poor sheep! What have you done?!”*

    **Way 4:** Talk to [Tunlon](../monsters/tunlon.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** killed 1× [Mountain Sheep](../monsters/bwm_sheep1.md); not reached stage 44 of [Blackwater Mountain story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/bwmfill_nondisplay.md#stage-44)
    - *“My poor sheep! What have you done?!”*

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 20; reached stage 50 of [A path to the Duleian Road](../quests/pathway_fallhaven.md#stage-50)
    - *“I have heard that a forest to the north was cleared out recently. Sure enough they got some wood laying around there.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

    - **Needs:** stage 20; not yet stage 30; reached stage 10 of [Devastated land](../quests/hadracor.md#stage-10)
    - *“Oh we do have a lot of wood indeed. However, all of this is very brittle and can only be used as firewood.”*


<span id="route-32"></span>

    - *“I like to believe that. Look, I told my men to cut down some trees for you. You can now take a shortcut.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Tunlon · 1 way"

    - **Needs:** stage 10, 20, 32; reached stage 21 of [Cheap cuts](../quests/benbyr.md#stage-21)
    - *“Please go and ask him, if he knows anyone who could help.”*

<span id="route-220"></span>

    - **Needs:** stage 220; not yet stage 230

<span id="route-235"></span>

<span id="route-240"></span>
