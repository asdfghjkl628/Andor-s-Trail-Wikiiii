## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Kaori, Grumpy Vilegard villager, Vilegard woman, Tharwyn, Vi · 6 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Kaori](../monsters/kaori.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so afraid of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't want to talk to you. Go talk to Jolnor in the chapel if you want to help us.”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Grumpy Vilegard villager](../monsters/grumpy_vilegard_villager.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so afraid of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't trust you. You should go see Jolnor in the chapel if you want some sympathy.”*

    **Way 3:** Talk to [Vilegard woman](../monsters/vilegard_woman.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so afraid of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't trust you. You should go see Jolnor in the chapel if you want some sympathy.”*

    **Way 4:** Talk to [Tharwyn](../monsters/tharwyn.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so suspicious of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't trust you. You should go see Jolnor in the chapel if you want some sympathy.”*

    **Way 5:** Talk to [Vilegard armorer](../monsters/vilegard_armorer.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so suspicious of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't trust you. You should go see Jolnor in the chapel if you want some sympathy.”*

    **Way 6:** Talk to [Vilegard smith](../monsters/vilegard_smith.md), choose “Why is everyone in Vilegard so suspicious of outsiders?”

    - *“I don't trust you. You should go see Jolnor in the chapel if you want some sympathy.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Jolnor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Jolnor](../monsters/jolnor.md), choose “No, but I am working on it.”

    - **Needs:** stage 20
    - *“So, in order to gain our trust here in Vilegard, I would suggest you help Kaori, Wrye and me.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Jolnor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Jolnor](../monsters/jolnor.md), choose “I have done all the tasks you asked me to do.”

    - **Needs:** stage 20; reached stage 30 of [Spies in the foam](../quests/jolnor.md#stage-30); reached stage 20 of [Kaori's errands](../quests/kaori.md#stage-20); reached stage 90 of [Uncertain cause](../quests/wrye.md#stage-90)
    - *“Good. You helped all three of us.”*
