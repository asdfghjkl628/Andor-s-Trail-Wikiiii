    Overview¶

    Anoa told me that my presence in this cavern of Undertell had reawakened 11 shades of the Kha'zaan, who were dormant. I needed to eliminate them before they destroyed Dhayavar. It was their devotion to Kazaul which had preserved their existence till now.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Anoa (Undertell 3 02). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 30 of Devotion
    • NOT reached stage 20 of Devotion
