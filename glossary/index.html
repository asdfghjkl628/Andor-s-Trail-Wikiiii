    Glossary¶

    Short forms used on this wiki and by the community because typing “Polished ring of backstabbing” every time gets old. Community short forms are from the forum thread Common Acronyms and Abbreviations.

    Stats¶

    Short Stands for Meaning
    AC Attack chance Your accuracy, compared with the target's BC. (Not "armor class".)
    AD Attack damage Damage range of an attack, min–max. Also written Dmg.
    AP Action points Spent on attacking, moving and using items in combat.
    BC Block chance Your evasion, compared with the attacker's AC.
    CM Critical multiplier Damage multiplier of a critical hit (e.g. ×2). Comes from the weapon.
    CS Critical skill Sets the crit chance. ⚠ In skill lists, CS means Combat Speed.
    Crit Critical hit A hit multiplied by CM.
    DR Damage resistance Subtracted from every hit you take.
    ECC Effective critical chance Your actual crit chance after the CS formula.
    HP Health points Current and max health.
    Lv Level Character level (or skill level).
    NPC Non-player character Someone you can talk to. Some can also be fought.
    XP / Exp Experience points From kills and quests; needed to level up.
    PV Places visited From the in-game statistics screen.
    FQ Finished quests Completed quests, from the in-game statistics screen.

    Skills¶

    On the forum, skills are written as short form:level, e.g. IF:4 = Increased Fortitude level 4. Proficiencies and fighting styles use a prefix: WP: weapon proficiency, AP: armor proficiency, FS: fighting style, S: specialization.

    Short Skill
    HH Hard Hit
    WA Weapon Accuracy
    D Dodge
    CS Combat Speed
    Cl Cleave
    IF Increased Fortitude
    Ev Evasion
    Re Regeneration
    WP:DA Dagger proficiency
    WP:1S One-handed sword proficiency
    WP:B Blunt weapon proficiency
    AP:L Light armor proficiency
    FS:DW Fighting style: Dual wield
    S:DW Specialization: Dual wield

    Reading a forum character summary¶

    Players often post their character like this:

    Level:75, XP:7656192, PV:866, FQ:105
HP:226, AC:255, AD:47-61, AP:3, ECC:23%, CM:3.0, BC:192, DR:3
HH:1, WA:1, D:1, CS:2, Cl:1, IF:4, Ev:3, Re:2, FS:DW:2, S:DW:1

    Line 1 is progress (level, XP, places visited, finished quests), line 2 is combat stats (AP here appears to be the attack cost, since max AP starts at 10), line 3 is skills with their levels. Equipment is listed with the item short forms above.

    Sources: Common Acronyms and Abbreviations, Andor's Trail forum.
