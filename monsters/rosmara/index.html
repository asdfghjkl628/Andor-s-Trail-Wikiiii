    Dialogue (20 lines)

    What the dialogue says, exactly as in the game files. Lines are listed once; links jump to the line a choice leads to.

    rosmara_initial_phrase Rosmara: “How can I help you today?”

    • “What is going on with that cat?” → rosmara_cat_1
    • “What is this place?” → rosmara_explain
    • “Do you know why the village of Wexlow is void of people?” (if reached stage 11 of feygard_nondisplayed (hidden flag); NOT reached stage 10 of Echoes of enchantment) → rosmara_wexlow
    • “I would like to make a purchase, please.” (if reached stage 8 of feygard_nondisplayed (hidden flag)) → shop opens

    rosmara_cat_1 Rosmara: “Well, you see, he's my guard cat.”

    • “Your guard cat?” → rosmara_cat_2

    rosmara_explain Rosmara: “This is my place of business. Right here on the side of Duleian Road.”

    • “Why here? There's nothing else here besides you.” → rosmara_explain_2
    • “What do you sell?” → rosmara_explain_3

    rosmara_wexlow Rosmara: “What?! There's nobody in the village?”

    • “Nope. Not a single person, and it looks like there hasn't been for a while.” → rosmara_wexlow2

    rosmara_cat_2 Rosmara: “Yes. If you attempt to rob me, he'll attack you. Hence he is my guard cat.”

    • “I see.” → conversation ends
    • “Seriously?” → rosmara_cat_3

    rosmara_explain_2 Rosmara: “Oh, you silly child. This is a very well traveled road. All the people coming and going from and to Feygard. It's a long road and they get hungry.”

    • “I'm not a child anymore!” (if wearing #heartsteel_filter) → rosmara_heartsteel_10
    • “I am not a child!” (if NOT carry 1× #heartsteel_filter) → conversation ends
    • “OK, you sell food. What kind of food?” → rosmara_explain_3
    • “And the fog? No one could take the path anymore.” → rosmara_explain_10

    rosmara_explain_3 Rosmara: “I sell things that a child such as yourself is most likely not interested in.”

    • “I am not a child anymore!!” → conversation ends
    • “What is it?” → rosmara_explain_4

    rosmara_wexlow2 Rosmara: “Hmm, I hope they are all right.”

    • “Me too.” → conversation ends

    rosmara_cat_3 Rosmara: “No, not seroiusly. He guards my fruits and vegetables.”

    • “Guards them from what?” → rosmara_cat_4

    rosmara_heartsteel_10 Rosmara: “Yes, yes you are and a foolhardy one at that.”

    • “Why the insult?” → rosmara_heartsteel_20

    rosmara_explain_10 Rosmara: “What kind of fog are you talking about?”

    • “The fog that the witch had created in the swamp, of course.” (if reached stage 30 of Fog in the woods) → rosmara_explain_11
    • “The fog further down the road behind the kobolds, of course.” (if NOT reached stage 30 of Fog in the woods) → rosmara_explain_11a

    rosmara_explain_4 Rosmara: “I sell fruits and vegetables.” — effects: sets stage 8 of feygard_nondisplayed (hidden flag)

    • “As an adult, I am interested in seeing what you have.” → shop opens
    • “Where do you get these fruits and vegetables?” → rosmara_explain_5

    rosmara_cat_4 Rosmara: “I have a business agreement with him. He guards my food from the rats in exchange for a place to sleep.”

    • “Oh, why didn't you just say so?” → rosmara_cat_5
    • “Well, he's not holding up his end of the deal, because there's a rat behind your tent right now.” → rosmara_cat_rat

    rosmara_heartsteel_20 Rosmara: “Carrying the very same weapon Geomyr banned?”

    • “Oh! Oops!” → conversation ends

    rosmara_explain_11 Rosmara: “Are you still afraid of witches or fog, child?”

    • “I am not a child anymore!!” → conversation ends
    • “I was with the witch! Without me the fog would still be there!” → rosmara_explain_12

    rosmara_explain_11a Rosmara: “Are you still afraid of kobolds or fog, child?”

    • “I am not a child anymore!!” → conversation ends
    • “I have mastered the fog and survived the kobolds!!” → rosmara_explain_12

    rosmara_explain_5 Rosmara: “From all over Dhayavar, but mostly from the great farmers in Loneford.”

    • “Oh, yeah. I've seen all of their great farms.” (if reached stage 22 of Flows through the veins) → conversation ends
    • “Loneford, where's that?” (if NOT reached stage 22 of Flows through the veins) → conversation ends

    rosmara_cat_5 Rosmara: “I wanted to waste your time just as you are doing to my time. Walking up to my business counter asking about my cat! Get lost, kid.”

    rosmara_cat_rat Rosmara: “Oh, my!”

    rosmara_explain_12 Rosmara: “Yes, of course. Calm down, child.”
