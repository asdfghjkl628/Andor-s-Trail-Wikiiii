    Elm mine3¶
    Map ID elm_mine3
    Type Indoors / underground
    Size 19×27 tiles
    World map Elmmine
    Introduced v0.7.14
    NPCs 1

    Elm mine3 is an indoor map. It has 1 NPC and 6 kinds of enemy. Exits lead to Elm mine2.

    Map¶
