    Overview¶

    Yolgen asked me to have a look at what is wrong with Flagstone prison.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Yolgen (stoutford_church). Required:

    • reached stage 80 of Rumblings
    • reached stage 180 of Placeholder for hidden quest stages 2 (not displayed) (hidden flag)
    • reached stage 190 of Placeholder for hidden quest stages 2 (not displayed) (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 10 of stoutford_reinforcements (hidden flag)
    • reached stage 10 of not_yet_realized (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 5 of Ancient secrets
    • NOT reached stage 60 of Ancient secrets
    • NOT reached stage 100 of Ancient secrets
