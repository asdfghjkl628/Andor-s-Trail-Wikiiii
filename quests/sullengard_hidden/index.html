    Sullengard story flags¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track progress. It does not appear in the journal. The stage descriptions below are internal notes written by the developers and may be brief.
    Quest ID sullengard_hidden
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 47
    Started by Godfrey (Sullengard inn)
    NPCs involved Deebo, Drinking brother, Drinking brother, Drinking brother, Frosty, Godfrey +10
    Locations Fallhaven derelict 2, Fallhaven derelict 2 t, Lake shore road 9, Mywild 20 houseleft
    Total XP 3,300
    Related quests 13

    Overview¶

    Bought a Sullengard bed.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Godfrey (Sullengard inn). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 1 of Sullengard story flags (hidden flag)
    • pay 700 gold

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
