    Overview¶

    Off the main road, southeast of Deebo's Orchard, I've stumbled across a group of sketchy looking men with familiar voices.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on Aidem camp. Required:

    • NOT reached stage 10 of Wanted men
    • reached stage 39 of Sullengard story flags (hidden flag)
