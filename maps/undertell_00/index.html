    Undertell 00¶
    Map ID undertell_00
    Type Indoors / underground
    Size 30×30 tiles
    World map Undertell floor 1
    Introduced v0.8.18
    NPCs 1
    Enemy types 4
    Quests 2

    Undertell 00 is an indoor map. It has 1 NPC and 4 kinds of enemy. Exits lead to Undertell 10, Undertell 3 lava 00.
