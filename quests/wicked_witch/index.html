    A Wicked witch¶
    Quest ID wicked_witch
    In journal Yes
    Stages 14 (completes at 50, 70, 95, 96)
    Started by Bela
    NPCs involved Bela, Bonicksa, Bonicksa, Busy farmer, Emmeline
    Locations Fallhaven south-east, Lake shore road 1, Witch house
    Total XP 46,182
    Related quests 2

    Overview¶

    Bela, the Fallhaven tavern keeper heard from a customer about a possible kidnapping of a girl. I should ask around town if anyone knows more.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Bela. Required:

    • reached stage 90 of A giant snake
    • NOT reached stage 50 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 70 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 95 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 96 of A Wicked witch

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 10 · Bela · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Bela, choose “So?”

    • Needs: not yet stage 50, 70, 95, 96; reached stage 90 of A giant snake
    • “A customer with a very interesting report about a kidnapped girl and a witch.”

    Stage 20 · Busy farmer · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Busy farmer, choose “Great, more stories with grandpa.”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 40
    • “She was a young beautiful woman, but terrifyingly alluring. So much so, that she easily coerced us into following her into her house.”

    Stage 30 · Busy farmer · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Busy farmer, choose “You think?”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 40
    • “Yes. You see, after I escaped, I never saw Addie again.”

    Stage 40 · Busy farmer · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Busy farmer, choose “Thank you for telling me your story. I want to stop this witch once and for all. Where can I find her?”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 40
    • “Thank you! You can find her house just south of here. You can't miss it as it is covered in beautiful flowers.”

    Stage 50 · Bonicksa · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Bonicksa, choose “Witches don't bother me. I'll leave you be.”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 40
    • Also: starts timer “wicked_witch_despawn_timer”
    • “How intriguing. Such confidence in your path. We shall see, won't we?”

    Stage 60 · stepping on a trigger on witch_house · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Witch house

    • Needs: not yet stage 60; killed 1× Bonicksa
    • Gives: spawns monsters on witch_house
    • “As the "witch" dies, she begins to transform into her true form, revealing a young girl and you quickly realize the tragedy of your…”

    Stage 65 · stepping on a trigger on witch_house · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Witch house

    • Needs: not yet stage 65; killed 1× Bonicksa
    • Gives: spawns monsters on witch_house
    • “After defeating the real witch, she quickly reappears.”

    Stage 70 · Bonicksa · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Bonicksa, choose “[disgruntled] Is this some kind of sick game to you?”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 65
    • Gives: spawns monsters on lake_shore_road_0, changes map lake_shore_road_0
    • Also: sets stage 1 of Sutdover story flags (hidden flag)
    • “[smirks] Perhaps. But remember, hero, life's full of surprises. You may have won this time, but who's to say what lies ahead?”

    Stage 80 · Emmeline · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, choose “What ... do I know you? You're just a young girl?”

    • Needs: not yet stage 90, 95, 96
    • Gives: spawns monsters on lake_shore_road_0, changes map lake_shore_road_0
    • Also: sets stage 1 of Sutdover story flags (hidden flag)
    • “Yes, I was under a spell that made me appear as the witch. She wanted to test your heart. I'm glad you proved kind.”

    Stage 85 · Emmeline · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, automatic

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 90
    • “I really need to get my hands on a lot of those 'Tonic of Blood' potions. Can you help me? I need a lot.”

    Stage 90 · Emmeline · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, choose “Can you help me out a little bit? Do you know anything about them?”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 90; carry 10× Tonic of blood; not carry 20× Tonic of blood
    • “Ah, YES. I remember her saying that an "undead" friend of her's east of here taught her how to make them.”

    Stage 95 · Emmeline · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, choose “Well, I have twenty-five of those for you.”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 90; hand over 25× Tonic of blood
    • “WOW! You actually managed to find twenty-five! I am so grateful.”

    Stage 96 · Emmeline · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, choose “I know that you "asked" for twenty-five, but I have twenty and they are hard to get. Please take these.”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 90; carry 20× Tonic of blood; not carry 25× Tonic of blood; hand over 20× Tonic of blood
    • “OK. I won't make you go back just for five more when you've already brought me twenty.”
