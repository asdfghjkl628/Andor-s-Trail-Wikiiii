## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “Anything for the glorious Feygard.”

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 10
    - *“Great to hear. I need you to talk with the tavern owner and find out anything you can about the beer.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Torilo · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Torilo](../monsters/torilo.md), choose “What does that mean?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-10) is 10; pay 10,000 gold
    - *“It means that that is all I will tell you and I suggest you talk to another tavern owner.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Tharwyn · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tharwyn](../monsters/tharwyn.md), choose “The thief?”

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 30; reached stage 30 of [Trusting an outsider](../quests/vilegard.md#stage-30); reached stage 32 of [Sullengard story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/sullengard_hidden.md#stage-32)
    - *“That is Dunla. He gets me my beer. Go talk to him.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Dunla · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Dunla](../monsters/dunla.md), choose “Is the Thieves guild the 'distributors'?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-30) is 30; reached stage 50 of [The ruthless Crackshot](../quests/Thieves03.md#stage-50)
    - *“Yes, yes we are, but if you want to learn more, go talk to Farrik back at our guild house.”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Farrik · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Farrik](../monsters/farrik.md), choose “Yes, it was 'silly' of you to do that.”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 70 of [Night visit](../quests/farrik.md#stage-70); latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-40) is 40
    - *“It is Sullengard, of course.”*


<span id="route-60"></span>

??? note "Stage 60 · Mayor Ale · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Mayor Ale](../monsters/sullengard_mayor.md), choose “I guess you could say that.”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-50) is 50
    - **Gives:** 1× [Mayor Ale's letter](../items/sullengard_mayor_letter.md)
    - *“But how can you prove it to me?...Uh, I know. You can deliver this letter for me. Here, take it to Kealwea and return to me afterwards.”*


<span id="route-70"></span>

??? note "Stage 70 · Kealwea · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Kealwea](../monsters/sullengard_priest.md), choose “Mayor Ale has asked me to give this letter to you.”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 91 of [Mt. Galmore exploded star (hidden flag)](../quests/mg2_exploded_star_nd.md#stage-91); latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-60) is 60; hand over 1× [Mayor Ale's letter](../items/sullengard_mayor_letter.md)
    - *“Oh, I've been expecting this. Thank you.”*


<span id="route-80"></span>

??? note "Stage 80 · Mayor Ale · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Mayor Ale](../monsters/sullengard_mayor.md), choose “I can't argue with that logic.”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-70) is 70
    - *“Please keep this information to yourself.”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Mayor Ale](../monsters/sullengard_mayor.md), choose “But you are breaking the laws of Feygard. Aren't you afraid you will get caught?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-70) is 70
    - *“Not as much as we fear not being able to make a living selling our beer. Plus, we have the support of the people of Nor City”*


<span id="route-90"></span>

??? note "Stage 90 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “All that work, and the reward is just gold?”

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-80) is 80
    - *“Anyways, I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”*


<span id="route-100"></span>

??? note "Stage 100 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “Thanks and Shadow be with you.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-80) is 80
    - *“I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”*


<span id="route-110"></span>

??? note "Stage 110 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “Thanks!”

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-80) is 80
    - *“I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”*


<span id="route-120"></span>

??? note "Stage 120 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “That's the thing, I don't 'know' anything that I could prove.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of [Beer Bootlegging](../quests/beer_bootlegging.md#stage-80) is 80
    - **Gives:** faction “factionCountShadow” set to 4, faction “factionCountThieves” set to 4, faction “factionCountFeygard” set to -4
    - *“I knew that you would be a waste of my time. I'll reward you with nothing.”*
