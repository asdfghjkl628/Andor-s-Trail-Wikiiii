# Regeneration

*Gain +1 health points (HP) on every round per skill level when no monsters are directly adjacent.*

**Category:** immunity · **Max level:** unlimited · **Obtained via:** Skill points

## Requirements

- Max HP of at least 30 from level-ups (gear and skills don't count); each further level needs 30 more
- [Increased Fortitude](../skills/fortitude.md) level 1 (each further level needs 1 more)

## Description

Gain +1 health points (HP) on every round per skill level when no monsters are directly adjacent.
