    Stage 20 · Hadena · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Hadena, choose “I'm sorry because I was only half listening to you earlier, so I am a little fuzzy on the details. But can…”

    • Needs: not yet stage 10; latest stage of Getting home on time is 10

    • Needs: not yet stage 50; latest stage of Getting home on time is 20
    • “Anyways, I would really appreciate it if you would go to Loneford and get the pitchfork.”

    Stage 30 · Throthaus · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Throthaus, choose “No. But I'm a child of an ordinary farmer in a small settlement called Crossglen.”

    • Needs: latest stage of Getting home on time is 25
    • “If you are able to pull it out from the haystack, then it will be yours.”

    Stage 40 · walking into a blocked passage on loneford13 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Loneford 13, choose “Time to prove that I'm the child of farmer!”

    • Needs: stage 30
    • Gives: 1× Farmer's pitchfork, applies condition fatigue4
    • Also: sets stage 100 of Sullengard story flags (hidden flag)
    • “After several minutes of intense pulling you finally pull out the new pitchfork from the haystack.”

    • “Thank you so much, kid. Tell my wife Hadena I can come home on time today.”

    • Needs: not yet stage 60; latest stage of Getting home on time is 50
    • “Thank you so much for helping us. You are just like your brother.”
