    Overview¶

    Two Elytharan slave ghosts spoke quietly of another who has not joined them at the table. They said she still hides east of them, afraid the Shades might return.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Liberated Elytharan ghost (Undertell 3 00). Required:

    • reached stage 10 of About a girl
    • NOT reached stage 20 of About a girl

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 10 · Liberated Elytharan ghost · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Liberated Elytharan ghost, automatic

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 20
    • “There is another. A girl. She never came back here when the shades fell. Instead, she hides east of here. Probably in a corner somewhere.”

    Stage 20 · Liberated Elytharan ghost · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Liberated Elytharan ghost, choose “What did she make?”

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 20
    • “A folded coin. Copper. She believed evil could be trapped if it could not breathe.”

    Stage 30 · Liberated Elytharan ghost · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Liberated Elytharan ghost, choose “I am still trying to understand what happened to the talisman.”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 30
    • “They used to gather our things. Count them. Decide what mattered.”

    Stage 40 · Liberated Elytharan ghost · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Liberated Elytharan ghost, choose “Then where should I look?”

    • Needs: latest stage of About a girl is 30
    • Gives: spawns monsters on undertell_3_12
    • “With those who believed fear could be shaped. Go to the Masters.”

    Stage 50 · Thalen · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Thalen, choose “Where do I find it?”

    • Needs: stage 40; not yet stage 50; reached stage 90 of The fifth master
    • “In the lower records. Where discarded faith is kept for study, not mercy.”

    Stage 60 · walking into a blocked passage on undertell_archive2 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Undertell archive 2, choose “[Take the folded copper piece.]”

    • Needs: not yet stage 60
    • Gives: 1× Folded copper talisman
    • “You grab the coin off the shelf.”

    Stage 70 · walking into a blocked passage on undertell_3_12 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Undertell 3 12

    • Needs: stage 40; not yet stage 70
    • “THIS PERSON IS TRYING TO DESTROY ME!”

    Stage 80 · walking into a blocked passage on undertell_3_12 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Undertell 3 12, choose “I will meet you there.”

    • Needs: stage 70; not yet stage 80; reached stage 85 of Undertell story flags (hidden flag)
    • Gives: removes monsters from undertell_3_12, spawns monsters on undertell_3_00
    • “My name is Syrra. Thank you for bringing it back.”

    Stage 90 · Liberated Elytharan ghost · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Liberated Elytharan ghost, choose “Then this is enough.”

    • Needs: stage 80; not yet stage 90
    • “It is.”
