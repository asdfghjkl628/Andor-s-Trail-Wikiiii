    Version note

    As of v0.8.18, this quest cannot be completed: it had no ending yet. This has been the case since v0.7.13.

    Overview¶

    Mikhail gave me a book in which I could put down my most unusual experiences and achievements.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Mikhail (Home). Required:

    • reached stage 10 of Breakfast bread
    • NOT reached stage 1 of General story flags (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 1 of Unusual experiences and achievements
    • reached stage 100 of Breakfast bread
    • reached stage 100 of Rats!

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
