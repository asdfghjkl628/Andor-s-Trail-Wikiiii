    Overview¶

    I traded 5 of my 'mermaid coins' to Gylew for 500 gold.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Gylew (waterway5). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 100 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 105 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 20 of The odd coin collector
    • reached stage 220 of The silver scale
    • have 5 gold
    • NOT reached stage 10 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 11 of The odd coin collector
    • pay 5 gold
