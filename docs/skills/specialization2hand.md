# Specialization: Two-handed weapon

*Increases damage potential of two-handed weapons by an additional 50 % of the original damage potential, in addition to the benefits given by the weapon style skill.*

**Category:** specialty · **Max level:** 1 · **Obtained via:** Skill points

## Requirements

- Character level 45
- [Fighting style: Two-handed weapon](../skills/fightstyle2hand.md) level 2

## Description

Increases damage potential of two-handed weapons by an additional 50 % of the original damage potential, in addition to the benefits given by the weapon style skill. The attack chances of two-handed weapons are also increased by 20 % of their original attack chances.
