    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 20 · Ambelie · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Ambelie, choose “(Knock her out) Time to sleep!”

    • Needs: reached stage 15 of Immaculate kidnapping
    • Gives: sets stage 20 of Immaculate kidnapping, removes monsters from foaming_flask, spawns monsters on road1, applies condition carrying_ambelie
    • “(You tap her on the back of the head with the handle of your weapon, and she falls unconscious)”

    Way 2: Talk to Ambelie, choose “Give me something of value, and you won't see me again.”

    • Needs: reached stage 21 of Immaculate kidnapping
    • Gives: 1× Sapphire Necklace, sets stage 24 of Immaculate kidnapping, spawns monsters on road1
    • “Take this and leave me, please.”

    Stage 30 · stepping on a trigger on crackshot_hideout3 · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Crackshot hideout 3, choose “What are you talking about?”

    • Needs: killed 1× Crackshot; not reached stage 40 of The ruthless Crackshot
    • Gives: sets stage 40 of The ruthless Crackshot, removes monsters from road1
    • Also: clears stage 20 of Thieves story flags (hidden flag)
    • “The key is cursed .... She ... I am ... (Crackshot finally dies, emitting a soft bluish breath)”

    Stage 40 · Dying patrol · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Dying patrol, automatic

    • Needs: not yet stage 50
    • Gives: removes monsters from crackshot_hideout2
    • “(You see, horrified, how this man is bleeding out rapidly. He has countless cuts and his face is mutilated. You can almost hear his gasps.)”

    Stage 50 · Dying Patrol · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Dying Patrol, choose “What guy?”

    • Needs: stage 40
    • Gives: removes monsters from crackshot_hideout2
    • “No ... he's not .... Agggh! [He has stopped breathing. I cannot do anything for him. Better to move on.]”

    Stage 60 · stepping on a trigger on crackshot_hideout3 · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Crackshot hideout 3

    Stage 70 · walking into a blocked passage on crackshot_hideout3 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Crackshot hideout 3, choose “Insert the blessed key of Luthor into the lock.”

    • Needs: not reached stage 210 of Troubling times; carry 1× Blessed key of luthor
    • “The door opens.”

    Stage 80 · Thoronir · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Thoronir, choose “Fanamor, a member of Thieves' Guild, is severely wounded. Please give me a bandage for her!”

    • Needs: reached stage 18 of Score counters (hidden flag); reached stage 45 of Thief apprentice; reached stage 100 of Disallowed substance; not reached stage 50 of Thief apprentice

    Stage 90 · Feygard patrol sergeant · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol sergeant, automatic

    • Needs: killed 1× Crackshot
    • Gives: removes monsters from crackshot_hideout3
    • “You ... Argh ... [The sergeant takes one final breath and then dies. You should have come earlier. Now you will never get to know what he…”

    Stage 100 · Troublemaker · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “I've brought all the journals.”

    • Needs: reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; not reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice; reached stage 55 of Thief apprentice; hand over 1× Dunla's Journal; hand over 1× Fanamor's Journal; hand over 1× Leta's Journal
    • “Well done kid! You can now consider yourself skilled enough to be a part of this guild.”

    Way 2: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “I have the journals, but one of your spies, Fanamor, was killed by a Feygard scout.”

    • Needs: reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; not reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice; latest stage of Thief apprentice is 51; hand over 1× Dunla's Journal; hand over 1× Fanamor's Journal; hand over 1× Leta's Journal
    • “Well, that is the price of being one of us. There's always risk.”

    Stage 110 · Troublemaker · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “I gave you the journals, so where's my reward?”

    • Needs: stage 100; not yet stage 110; reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; not reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice
    • Gives: sets stage 60 of Thief apprentice, 900× Gold coins
    • “You should talk with Umar. Maybe he has another task ... one that's more in your line of work, you know.”

    Verified against v0.8.18 dialogue data.
