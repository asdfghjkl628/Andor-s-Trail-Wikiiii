## Overview

> After giving him the key of Luthor and talking with Umar about Andor, I needed to ask him about Thieves' Guild itself. I want to join them to see what kind of jobs they do.

## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Umar](../monsters/umar.md) ([Fallhaven derelict 2](../maps/fallhaven_derelict2.md)). Required:

- reached stage 51 of [Search for Andor](../quests/andor.md#stage-51)
- reached stage 100 of [Key of Luthor](../quests/bucus.md#stage-100)
- NOT reached stage 5 of [Thief apprentice](../quests/Thieves01.md#stage-5)

## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-5"></span>

??? note "Stage 5 · Umar · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Umar](../monsters/umar.md), choose “But I really want to join your guild!”

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 5; reached stage 51 of [Search for Andor](../quests/andor.md#stage-51); reached stage 100 of [Key of Luthor](../quests/bucus.md#stage-100)
    - *“OK, OK. You will get a chance. But I warn you that from now on, you cannot go back on your choice.”*


<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Umar · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Umar](../monsters/umar.md), choose “I will take your opportunity!”

    - **Needs:** stage 5; not yet stage 10; reached stage 51 of [Search for Andor](../quests/andor.md#stage-51); not latest stage of [Thief apprentice](../quests/Thieves01.md#stage-60) is 60
    - *“Very well. Let's see if you're good enough to join our guild. Talk with Troublemaker. He will tell you what you have to do.”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Umar](../monsters/umar.md), choose “Anything else?”

    - **Needs:** stage 5; not yet stage 10; reached stage 51 of [Search for Andor](../quests/andor.md#stage-51); not latest stage of [Thief apprentice](../quests/Thieves01.md#stage-60) is 60
    - *“Not for now. Make sure nobody sees you doing suspicious things. Good luck.”*


<span id="route-15"></span>

??? note "Stage 15 · Troublemaker · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Troublemaker](../monsters/troublemaker.md), choose “Sounds easy, I'll do it.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10
    - *“Good. We have three people on the job. The first one is a spy in Crossglen, Leta. I believe you know her. One of our veteran spies, Dunla,…”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Troublemaker · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Troublemaker](../monsters/troublemaker.md), choose “Anything else?”

    - **Needs:** stage 10
    - *“Ah! I almost forgot. You have to say the password if you want them to give you the journal. The password is "You are no one. No one knows…”*


<span id="route-25"></span>

??? note "Stage 25 · Leta · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Leta](../monsters/leta.md), choose “Umar sent me to get your journal.”

    - **Needs:** stage 20; not yet stage 25
    - **Gives:** 1× [Leta's Journal](../items/Leta_journal.md)
    - *“Here. Make sure you don't raise any suspicion on your future jobs. Bye kid.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Dunla · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Dunla](../monsters/dunla.md), choose “You are no one. No one knows you. No one has seen you.”

    - **Needs:** stage 25; not yet stage 30
    - **Gives:** 1× [Dunla's Journal](../items/Dunla_journal.md)
    - *“So, you are one of us. Here, take my journal.”*


<span id="route-35"></span>

??? note "Stage 35 · Fanamor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Fanamor](../monsters/fanamor.md), choose “Umar sent me with the words "You are no one.No one knows you.No one has seen you." Now give me the journal…”

    - **Needs:** stage 30
    - **Gives:** spawns monsters on crossroads
    - *“Take this. All I've seen is written here ...”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Fanamor, Feygard scout · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Fanamor](../monsters/fanamor.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 35; not yet stage 40
    - *“(Grabs the book) What have we here? A lost kid trying to do business with this scum, hah? You are under arrest!”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Feygard scout](../monsters/feygard_scout.md), automatic

    - *“(Grabs the book) What have we here? A lost kid trying to do business with this scum, hah? You are under arrest!”*


<span id="route-45"></span>

??? note "Stage 45 · Fanamor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Fanamor](../monsters/fanamor.md), choose “Can I help?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40; killed 1× [Feygard scout](../monsters/feygard_scout.md)
    - <small>Also: starts timer “Fanamorbleeding”</small>
    - *“I need a bandage quickly, or I will never return to the guild house. A priest might help ...”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Thoronir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Thoronir](../monsters/thoronir.md), choose “Ehm ... My father has cut himself with an axe, and we don't have any bandages!”

    - **Needs:** stage 45; not yet stage 50; reached stage 18 of [Score counters (hidden flag)](../quests/scores.md#stage-18); reached stage 100 of [Disallowed substance](../quests/bonemeal.md#stage-100)
    - **Gives:** 1× [Bandage](../items/bandage.md)
    - *“This is what I have, take it. I expect this will be useful.”*


<span id="route-51"></span>

??? note "Stage 51 · Fanamor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Fanamor](../monsters/fanamor.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 45; 120 rounds passed since timer “Fanamorbleeding”
    - *“(You look away when you see the corpse of Fanamor. Anklebiters probably had something to do with this horrible event.)”*


<span id="route-55"></span>

??? note "Stage 55 · Fanamor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Fanamor](../monsters/fanamor.md), choose “Yes, I have it!”

    - **Needs:** stage 45, 50; hand over 1× [Bandage](../items/bandage.md)
    - **Gives:** removes monsters from crossroads, spawns monsters on fallhaven_derelict2
    - *“(You help Fanamor put the bandage on the wound) ... I think I will be able ... to move in a few minutes. See you at the Guild. Thank you…”*


<span id="route-60"></span>

??? note "Stage 60 · Troublemaker · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Troublemaker](../monsters/troublemaker.md), choose “I gave you the journals, so where's my reward?”

    - **Needs:** stage 20; not yet stage 60; reached stage 100 of [Thieves story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/thieves_hidden.md#stage-100); not reached stage 110 of [Thieves story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/thieves_hidden.md#stage-110)
    - **Gives:** 900× [Gold coins](../items/gold.md)
    - <small>Also: sets stage 110 of [Thieves story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/thieves_hidden.md#stage-110)</small>
    - *“You should talk with Umar. Maybe he has another task ... one that's more in your line of work, you know.”*
