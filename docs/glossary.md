# Glossary

Short forms used on this wiki and by the community ~~because typing “Polished ring of backstabbing” every time gets old~~. Community short forms are from the forum thread [Common Acronyms and Abbreviations](https://andorstrail.com/viewtopic.php?t=7651).

## Stats

| Short | Stands for | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **AC** | [Attack chance](skills/stats.md#attack-chance) | Your accuracy, compared with the target's BC. (Not "armor class".) |
| **AD** | [Attack damage](skills/stats.md#attack-damage) | Damage range of an attack, min–max. Also written Dmg. |
| **AP** | [Action points](skills/stats.md#max-ap) | Spent on attacking, moving and using items in combat. |
| **BC** | [Block chance](skills/stats.md#block-chance) | Your evasion, compared with the attacker's AC. |
| **CM** | [Critical multiplier](skills/stats.md#critical-multiplier) | Damage multiplier of a critical hit (e.g. ×2). Comes from the weapon. |
| **CS** | [Critical skill](skills/stats.md#critical-skill) | Sets the crit chance. ⚠ In skill lists, CS means Combat Speed. |
| **Crit** | [Critical hit](skills/stats.md#critical-skill) | A hit multiplied by CM. |
| **DR** | [Damage resistance](skills/stats.md#damage-resistance) | Subtracted from every hit you take. |
| **ECC** | [Effective critical chance](skills/stats.md#critical-skill) | Your actual crit chance after the CS formula. |
| **HP** | [Health points](skills/stats.md#max-hp) | Current and max health. |
| **Lv** | [Level](skills/index.md) | Character level (or skill level). |
| **NPC** | [Non-player character](monsters/index.md) | Someone you can talk to. Some can also be fought. |
| **XP / Exp** | [Experience points](skills/index.md) | From kills and quests; needed to level up. |
| **PV** | Places visited | From the in-game statistics screen. |
| **FQ** | Finished quests | Completed quests, from the in-game statistics screen. |

## Skills

On the forum, skills are written as *short form:level*, e.g. `IF:4` = Increased Fortitude level 4. Proficiencies and fighting styles use a prefix: **WP:** weapon proficiency, **AP:** armor proficiency, **FS:** fighting style, **S:** specialization.

## Reading a forum character summary

Players often post their character like this:

```
Level:75, XP:7656192, PV:866, FQ:105
HP:226, AC:255, AD:47-61, AP:3, ECC:23%, CM:3.0, BC:192, DR:3
HH:1, WA:1, D:1, CS:2, Cl:1, IF:4, Ev:3, Re:2, FS:DW:2, S:DW:1
```

Line 1 is progress (level, XP, places visited, finished quests), line 2 is combat stats (*AP* here appears to be the attack cost, since max AP starts at 10), line 3 is skills with their levels. Equipment is listed with the item short forms above.

Sources: [Common Acronyms and Abbreviations](https://andorstrail.com/viewtopic.php?t=7651), Andor's Trail forum.
