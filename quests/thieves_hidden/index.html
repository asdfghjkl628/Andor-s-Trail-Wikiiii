    Thieves Hidden¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID thieves_hidden
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 11
    Started by Ambelie (foaming_flask), Ambelie (foaming_flask)
    NPCs involved Ambelie, Dying Patrol, Dying patrol, Feygard patrol sergeant, Thoronir, Troublemaker
    Locations crackshot_hideout2, crackshot_hideout3, fallhaven_church, fallhaven_derelict2
    Related quests 6

    How each stage is reached¶

    Every dialogue route found in the game data, including alternatives that end up in the same place. "Conditions" are everything checked along that dialogue path.

    Stage 20: 2 routes
    1. Talk to Ambelie (foaming_flask) → choose “(Knock her out) Time to sleep!” — conditions: reached stage 15 of Immaculate kidnapping → stage 20; also sets stage 20 of Immaculate kidnapping, removes monsters from foaming_flask, spawns monsters on road1, applies condition carrying_ambelie. NPC: “(You tap her on the back of the head with the handle of your weapon, and she falls unconscious)”
    2. Talk to Ambelie (foaming_flask) → choose “Give me something of value, and you won't see me again.” — conditions: reached stage 21 of Immaculate kidnapping → stage 20; also gives 1× Sapphire Necklace, sets stage 24 of Immaculate kidnapping, spawns monsters on road1. NPC: “Take this and leave me, please.”
    Stage 30: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on crackshot_hideout3 → choose “What are you talking about?” — conditions: killed 1× Crackshot; NOT reached stage 40 of The ruthless Crackshot → stage 30; also sets stage 40 of The ruthless Crackshot, clears stage 20 of Thieves Hidden (hidden flag), removes monsters from road1. NPC: “The key is cursed .... She ... I am ... (Crackshot finally dies, emitting a soft bluish breath)”
    Stage 40: 1 route
    1. Talk to Dying patrol (crackshot_hideout2) → the conversation leads here automatically — conditions: NOT reached stage 50 of Thieves Hidden (hidden flag) → stage 40; also removes monsters from crackshot_hideout2. NPC: “(You see, horrified, how this man is bleeding out rapidly. He has countless cuts and his face is mutilated. You can…”
    Stage 50: 1 route
    1. Talk to Dying Patrol (crackshot_hideout2) → choose “What guy?” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Thieves Hidden (hidden flag) → stage 50; also removes monsters from crackshot_hideout2. NPC: “No ... he's not .... Agggh! [He has stopped breathing. I cannot do anything for him. Better to move on.]”
    Stage 60: 1 route
    1. stepping on a trigger on crackshot_hideout3 → the conversation leads here automatically → stage 60
    Stage 70: 1 route
    1. walking into a blocked passage on crackshot_hideout3 → choose “Insert the blessed key of Luthor into the lock.” — conditions: NOT reached stage 210 of Troubling times; carry 1× Blessed key of luthor → stage 70. NPC: “The door opens.”
    Stage 80: 1 route
    1. Talk to Thoronir (fallhaven_church) → choose “Fanamor, a member of Thieves' Guild, is severely wounded. Please give me a bandage for her!” — conditions: reached stage 18 of scores (hidden flag); reached stage 45 of Thief apprentice; reached stage 100 of Disallowed substance; NOT reached stage 50 of Thief apprentice → stage 80
    Stage 90: 1 route
    1. Talk to Feygard patrol sergeant (crackshot_hideout3) → the conversation leads here automatically — conditions: killed 1× Crackshot → stage 90; also removes monsters from crackshot_hideout3. NPC: “You ... Argh ... [The sergeant takes one final breath and then dies. You should have come earlier. Now you will never…”
    Stage 100: 2 routes
    1. Talk to Troublemaker (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “I've brought all the journals.” — conditions: reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; NOT reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice; reached stage 55 of Thief apprentice; hand over 1× Dunla's Journal; hand over 1× Fanamor's Journal; hand over 1× Leta's Journal → stage 100. NPC: “Well done kid! You can now consider yourself skilled enough to be a part of this guild.”
    2. Talk to Troublemaker (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “I have the journals, but one of your spies, Fanamor, was killed by a Feygard scout.” — conditions: reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; NOT reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice; latest stage of Thief apprentice is 51; hand over 1× Dunla's Journal; hand over 1× Fanamor's Journal; hand over 1× Leta's Journal → stage 100. NPC: “Well, that is the price of being one of us. There's always risk.”
    Stage 110: 1 route
    1. Talk to Troublemaker (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “I gave you the journals, so where's my reward?” — conditions: reached stage 20 of Thief apprentice; NOT reached stage 60 of Thief apprentice; reached stage 100 of Thieves Hidden (hidden flag); NOT reached stage 110 of Thieves Hidden (hidden flag) → stage 110; also sets stage 60 of Thief apprentice, gives 900× Gold coins. NPC: “You should talk with Umar. Maybe he has another task ... one that's more in your line of work, you know.”

    Verified against v0.8.18 dialogue data.
