    hidden_undertell¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track progress. It does not appear in the journal. The stage descriptions below are internal notes written by the developers and may be brief.
    Quest ID undertell_hidden
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 39
    Started by walking into a blocked passage on galmore_58
    NPCs involved Alkapoan, Brenor, Drunkard, Lethgar miner ghost, Lethgar slave ghost, Morvath +8
    Locations brimhaven4, brimhaven_house1, fallhaven_nw, undertell_00
    Related quests 10

    Overview¶

    3 = PC has encountered the Galmore encampment barricades.

    None: talk to walking into a blocked passage on galmore_58 to begin.
