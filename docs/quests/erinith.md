## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “What's wrong?”

    - *“I was setting up camp here during the night, and was attacked by some bandits while asleep.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “I could help you find that book if you want.”

    - *“You would? Oh thank you.”*


<span id="route-21"></span>

??? note "Stage 21 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “200 gold it is then. I'll go look for your book.”

    - *“Make it quick.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “Yes, here is your book.”

    - **Needs:** stage 20; hand over 1× [Erinith's book](../items/erinith_book.md)


<span id="route-31"></span>

??? note "Stage 31 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 30
    - *“One of those would surely do. Otherwise, I think four regular potions of health would be enough.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “Here, take this bonemeal potion instead. It's very potent in healing deep wounds.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30; hand over 1× [Bonemeal potion](../items/bonemeal_potion.md)
    - *“Bonemeal potion? But ... but ... we are not allowed to use them since they are prohibited by Lord Geomyr.”*


<span id="route-41"></span>

??? note "Stage 41 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “Here, take this major potion of health.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30; hand over 1× [Major potion of health](../items/health_major2.md)
    - *“Thank you for bringing me one. [Drinks potion]”*


<span id="route-42"></span>

??? note "Stage 42 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), choose “Here, take these four regular potions of health.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30; hand over 4× [Regular potion of health](../items/health.md)
    - *“Thank you for bringing them to me. [Drinks all four potions]”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Erinith · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Erinith](../monsters/erinith.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 40
    - *“Thank you my friend for your help. My book is safe and my wound is healing. I hope our paths will cross again.”*
