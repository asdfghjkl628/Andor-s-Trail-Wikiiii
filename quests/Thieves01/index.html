    Thief apprentice¶
    Quest ID Thieves01
    In journal Yes
    Stages 13 (completes at 60)
    Started by Umar (Fallhaven derelict 2)
    NPCs involved Dunla, Fanamor, Feygard scout, Leta, Thoronir, Troublemaker +1
    Locations Crossroads, Fallhaven church, Fallhaven derelict 2, Fallhaven derelict 2 t
    Total XP 4,550
    Related quests 9

    Overview¶

    After giving him the key of Luthor and talking with Umar about Andor, I needed to ask him about Thieves' Guild itself. I want to join them to see what kind of jobs they do.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Umar (Fallhaven derelict 2). Required:

    • reached stage 51 of Search for Andor
    • reached stage 100 of Key of Luthor
    • NOT reached stage 5 of Thief apprentice

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 5 · Umar · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Umar, choose “But I really want to join your guild!”

    • Needs: not yet stage 5; reached stage 51 of Search for Andor; reached stage 100 of Key of Luthor
    • “OK, OK. You will get a chance. But I warn you that from now on, you cannot go back on your choice.”

    Stage 10 · Umar · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Umar, choose “I will take your opportunity!”

    • Needs: stage 5; not yet stage 10; reached stage 51 of Search for Andor; not latest stage of Thief apprentice is 60
    • “Very well. Let's see if you're good enough to join our guild. Talk with Troublemaker. He will tell you what you have to do.”

    Way 2: Talk to Umar, choose “Anything else?”

    • Needs: stage 5; not yet stage 10; reached stage 51 of Search for Andor; not latest stage of Thief apprentice is 60
    • “Not for now. Make sure nobody sees you doing suspicious things. Good luck.”

    Stage 15 · Troublemaker · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “Sounds easy, I'll do it.”

    • Needs: stage 10
    • “Good. We have three people on the job. The first one is a spy in Crossglen, Leta. I believe you know her. One of our veteran spies, Dunla,…”

    Stage 20 · Troublemaker · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “Anything else?”

    • Needs: stage 10
    • “Ah! I almost forgot. You have to say the password if you want them to give you the journal. The password is "You are no one. No one knows…”

    Stage 25 · Leta · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Leta, choose “Umar sent me to get your journal.”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 25
    • Gives: 1× Leta's Journal
    • “Here. Make sure you don't raise any suspicion on your future jobs. Bye kid.”

    Stage 30 · Dunla · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Dunla, choose “You are no one. No one knows you. No one has seen you.”

    • Needs: stage 25; not yet stage 30
    • Gives: 1× Dunla's Journal
    • “So, you are one of us. Here, take my journal.”

    Stage 35 · Fanamor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Fanamor, choose “Umar sent me with the words "You are no one.No one knows you.No one has seen you." Now give me the journal…”

    • Needs: stage 30
    • Gives: spawns monsters on crossroads
    • “Take this. All I've seen is written here ...”

    Stage 40 · Fanamor, Feygard scout · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Fanamor, automatic

    • Needs: stage 35; not yet stage 40
    • “(Grabs the book) What have we here? A lost kid trying to do business with this scum, hah? You are under arrest!”

    Way 2: Talk to Feygard scout, automatic

    • “(Grabs the book) What have we here? A lost kid trying to do business with this scum, hah? You are under arrest!”

    Stage 45 · Fanamor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Fanamor, choose “Can I help?”

    • Needs: stage 40; killed 1× Feygard scout
    • Also: starts timer “Fanamorbleeding”
    • “I need a bandage quickly, or I will never return to the guild house. A priest might help ...”

    Stage 50 · Thoronir · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Thoronir, choose “Ehm ... My father has cut himself with an axe, and we don't have any bandages!”

    • Needs: stage 45; not yet stage 50; reached stage 18 of Score counters (hidden flag); reached stage 100 of Disallowed substance
    • Gives: 1× Bandage
    • “This is what I have, take it. I expect this will be useful.”

    Stage 51 · Fanamor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Fanamor, automatic

    • Needs: stage 45; 120 rounds passed since timer “Fanamorbleeding”
    • “(You look away when you see the corpse of Fanamor. Anklebiters probably had something to do with this horrible event.)”

    Stage 55 · Fanamor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Fanamor, choose “Yes, I have it!”

    • Needs: stage 45, 50; hand over 1× Bandage
    • Gives: removes monsters from crossroads, spawns monsters on fallhaven_derelict2
    • “(You help Fanamor put the bandage on the wound) ... I think I will be able ... to move in a few minutes. See you at the Guild. Thank you…”

    Stage 60 · Troublemaker · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Troublemaker, choose “I gave you the journals, so where's my reward?”

    • Needs: stage 20; not yet stage 60; reached stage 100 of Thieves story flags (hidden flag); not reached stage 110 of Thieves story flags (hidden flag)
    • Gives: 900× Gold coins
    • Also: sets stage 110 of Thieves story flags (hidden flag)
    • “You should talk with Umar. Maybe he has another task ... one that's more in your line of work, you know.”
