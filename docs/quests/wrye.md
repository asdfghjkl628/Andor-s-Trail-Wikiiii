## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Jolnor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Jolnor](../monsters/jolnor.md), choose “OK. Talk to Kaori. Got it.”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 10 of [Trusting an outsider](../quests/vilegard.md#stage-10)
    - *“She recently lost her son in a tragic way. If you can gain her trust, you will have a strong ally here.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “Can you tell me the story about what happened again?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40
    - *“I can feel it in me. The Shadow speaks to me. He is dead.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “What now?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40
    - *“I fear he has died or got hurt somehow. Those bastards probably drove him into his own death.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “What can I do to help?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40
    - *“If you want to help me, please find out what happened to my son, Rincel.”*


<span id="route-41"></span>

??? note "Stage 41 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “Any idea where I should look?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40
    - *“I guess you could ask in the tavern here in Vilegard, or the Foaming Flask tavern just north of here.”*


<span id="route-42"></span>

??? note "Stage 42 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Feygard patrol captain](../monsters/feygard_patrol_captain.md), choose “OK, that might be something worth checking anyway.”

    - **Needs:** stage 41
    - *“I noticed he left to the west heading out of the Foaming Flask tavern.”*


<span id="route-80"></span>

??? note "Stage 80 · Oluag · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Oluag](../monsters/oluag.md), choose “Maybe he didn't dare tell Wrye?”

    - **Needs:** stage 40, 80
    - *“So, anyway. Now he is buried over there. Rest in peace.”*


<span id="route-90"></span>

??? note "Stage 90 · Wrye · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Wrye](../monsters/wrye.md), choose “Yes, but not for long. He did not survive the wounds. He is now buried to the northwest of Vilegard.”

    - **Needs:** stage 40, 80
    - *“Oh my poor boy. What have I done?”*
