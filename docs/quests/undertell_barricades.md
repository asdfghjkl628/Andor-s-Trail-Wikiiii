## Overview

> I asked Myrelis how to get the barricades to Undertell removed and he told me to meet his friend Shannal inside the beach-scene house.

## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Myrelis](../monsters/mg_myrelis.md) ([Galmore 58](../maps/galmore_58.md)). Required:

- reached stage 9 of [Galmore story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/galmore_nondisplayed.md#stage-9)
- latest stage of [Lost treasures](../quests/nocmar.md#stage-35) is 35
- NOT reached stage 20 of [You shall pass](../quests/undertell_barricades.md#stage-20)

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 20; reached stage 9 of [Galmore story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/galmore_nondisplayed.md#stage-9); latest stage of [Lost treasures](../quests/nocmar.md#stage-35) is 35
    - **Gives:** spawns monsters on mt_galmore_railhouse
    - *“To enter Undertell, you need to talk to my friend Shannal, she has just come to spend a day at the beach. Shannal is the guardian ghost…”*

??? note "Stage 20 · Shannal · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Shannal](../monsters/shannal.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [You shall pass](../quests/undertell_barricades.md#stage-20) is 20
    - *“If you want to get into Undertell, you shall have to prove your capability to go inside. There are plenty of ghosts here. I don't want you…”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Shannal · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Shannal](../monsters/shannal.md), choose “So, what do you want from me?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [You shall pass](../quests/undertell_barricades.md#stage-20) is 20
    - **Gives:** 1× [Trader's Guild bronze coin](../items/trader_guild-coins.md)
    - *“Here, take this bronze coin. It is a 'Traders' Guild Bronze coin' and the only one I have left, so don't lose it. Now, go show it to…”*

    - *“Oh well, we're not proud of it. My ancestor was one of those who would force or capture people to work in the mines of Undertell. He would…”*

    - **Needs:** reached stage 90 of [A giant snake](../quests/bela_gsnake.md#stage-90); latest stage of [You shall pass](../quests/undertell_barricades.md#stage-140) is 140; pay 4,850 gold
    - **Gives:** removes monsters from fallhaven_nw, spawns monsters on fallhaven_nw
