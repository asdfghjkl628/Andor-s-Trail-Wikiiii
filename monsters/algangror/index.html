    Algangror¶

    Where to find Algangror: Lonelyhouse 0, Island 4 cave 1, Final cave 1, Final cave 2

    ![](../../assets/icons/monsters/monsters_rltiles1_68.png){ .sprite }
    Type NPC/Enemy (talks, but can also be fought)
    Role Starts Of mice and men, The five idols
    Found in Lonelyhouse 0, Island 4 cave 1, Final cave 1, Final cave 2
    Class Humanoid
    HP 200–241
    XP when defeated 258–548
    Introduced v0.7.0 or earlier

    Lonelyhouse 0¶

    Where: Lonelyhouse 0 · Role: Starts Of mice and men, The five idols

    You can fight Algangror

    The conversation during What is that stench? can lead straight into a fight with Algangror.

    Combat¶
    Class Humanoid
    HP 241
    XP when defeated 548
    Damage 3 to 9
    AC 80
    BC 120
    DR 4
    Attacks per turn 3 (3 AP each, 10 AP)
    Crit chance 58% (×2.0)

    Verified against v0.8.18 monster data.

    Drops¶

    Item Chance Qty
    Algangror's ring 100% 1
    Gold coins 100% 0 to 20
    Regular potion of health 100% 1 to 2
    Sharpened gem 100% 1
    Empty vial 100% 3 to 5

    Quests that count defeats¶

    • Final cave (hidden flag) with stepping on a trigger on Island 4 checks that this enemy has been defeated.

    Quests¶

    • Of mice and men: stages 10, 11, 15, 20, 21, 100, 101
    • The five idols: stages 10, 20, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 37, 51, 60, 61, 70, 100
    • What is that stench?: stages 30, 35

    How the XP value is calculated

    The game computes each enemy's experience value when it loads the data (MonsterTypeParser.java):

    XP = ⌈(attacks per turn × attack chance × average damage × (1 + critical skill × critical multiplier) × 3 + HP × (1 + block chance) + 9 × damage resistance) × 0.7⌉

    Percentages are used as fractions (e.g. 60% = 0.6). Enemies whose attacks inflict a condition are worth 50 XP more. The More Exp skill adds a percentage on top.
