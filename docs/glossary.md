# Glossary

Abbreviations used across this wiki. Most are also common in the Andor's Trail community. Each statistic is explained in full in the [stat glossary](skills/stats.md).

| Abbreviation | Stands for | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **AC** | [Attack chance](skills/stats.md#attack-chance) | Your accuracy; compared with the target's BC to decide whether an attack hits. (Not "armor class".) |
| **AP** | [Action points](skills/stats.md#max-ap) | Points spent on attacking, moving and using items during a combat turn. |
| **BC** | [Block chance](skills/stats.md#block-chance) | Your evasion; compared with the attacker's AC. |
| **CM** | [Critical multiplier](skills/stats.md#critical-multiplier) | How much a critical hit multiplies damage (e.g. ×2). Comes from the weapon. |
| **CS** | [Critical skill](skills/stats.md#critical-skill) | Determines the chance of a critical hit. |
| **Dmg** | [Attack damage](skills/stats.md#attack-damage) | The damage range of an attack (minimum to maximum). |
| **DR** | [Damage resistance](skills/stats.md#damage-resistance) | Subtracted from every hit you take. |
| **HP** | [Health points](skills/stats.md#max-hp) | Current and maximum health. |
| **Lv** | [Level](skills/index.md) | Character level (or skill level, where stated). |
| **NPC** | [Non-player character](monsters/index.md) | A character you can talk to. Some can also be fought (NPC/Enemy). |
| **XP / Exp** | [Experience points](skills/index.md) | Earned by defeating enemies and completing quests; needed to level up. |
| **Crit** | [Critical hit](skills/stats.md#critical-skill) | A hit whose damage is multiplied by the CM. |

**Percentages and points.** Skill descriptions that say "+20% of weapon AC" mean 20% of the bonus that item itself provides. "+9 BC" means nine points of block chance.
