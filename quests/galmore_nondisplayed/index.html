    galmore_nondisplayed¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID galmore_nondisplayed
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 46
    Started by stepping on a trigger on galmore_10, stepping on a trigger on galmore_12a
    NPCs involved Alkapoan, Celdar, Dark spirit, Egrinda, Eryndor, Leta's son +3
    Locations brimhaven_house1, crossglen_farmhouse_basement, galmore_17_house, galmore_32
    Total XP 12,000
    Related quests 9

    level 1 warning - marked stone

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Route 1 (stepping on a trigger on galmore_10):

    • affected by pull_of_the_mark
    • NOT reached stage 1 of galmore_nondisplayed (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 50 of A familiar shadow

    Route 2 (walking into a blocked passage on galmore_32):

    • nothing

    Route 3 (walking into a blocked passage on crossglen):

    • affected by pull_of_the_mark
    • NOT reached stage 1 of galmore_nondisplayed (hidden flag)
