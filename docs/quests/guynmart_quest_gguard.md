## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-1"></span>

??? note "Stage 1 · Guynmart guard · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Guynmart guard](../monsters/guynmart_gguard.md), choose “Here is 100 gold.”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 30 of [Roses](../quests/guynmart.md#stage-30); pay 100 gold
    - **Gives:** sets stage 30 of [Roses](../quests/guynmart.md#stage-30)


<span id="route-82"></span>

??? note "Stage 82 · Guynmart guard · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Guynmart guard](../monsters/guynmart_gguard.md), choose “I understand. Here, 100 gold.”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 70 of [Roses](../quests/guynmart.md#stage-70); not carry 1× [Rose](../items/guynmart_rose.md); not reached stage 100 of [Roses](../quests/guynmart.md#stage-100); not reached stage 132 of [Roses](../quests/guynmart.md#stage-132); have 100 gold; pay 100 gold
    - **Gives:** [Rose](../items/guynmart_rose.md)
    - *“And here is the rose. Don't tell anybody that you got it from me.”*
