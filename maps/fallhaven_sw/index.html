    Map ID fallhaven_sw

    Fallhaven south-west is an outdoor map, in Fallhaven (settlement). It has 4 NPCs and 2 kinds of enemy. Exits lead to Fallhaven north-west, Fallhaven south-east, Wild10, Wild9 and 3 more.
