> Leonid in Crossglen town hall tells me that there was a disturbance in the village some weeks ago. Apparently, Lord Geomyr has banned all use of bonemeal as a healing substance. Tharal, the town priest should know more.

## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Leonid · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Leonid](../monsters/leonid.md), choose “Has there been any recent activity in the village?”

    - *“Lord Geomyr issued a statement regarding the unlawful use of bonemeal as healing substance. Some villagers argued that we should oppose…”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Tharal · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tharal](../monsters/tharal.md), choose “Oh come on.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10
    - *“Well if you really are that persistent. Bring me 5 insect wings that I can use for making potions and maybe we can talk more.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Tharal · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tharal](../monsters/tharal.md), choose “Here, I have the insect wings.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10; hand over 5× [Insect wing](../items/insectwing.md)
    - *“Thanks kid. I knew I could count on you.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Thoronir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Thoronir](../monsters/thoronir.md), choose “Sure, I might be able to do that.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30; reached stage 18 of [Score counters (hidden flag)](../quests/scores.md#stage-18)
    - *“Thank you, please come back soon. I heard there were some undead near an old abandoned house just north of Fallhaven. Maybe you can check…”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Thoronir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Thoronir](../monsters/thoronir.md), choose “Sure, I might be able to do that.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30, 50; reached stage 18 of [Score counters (hidden flag)](../quests/scores.md#stage-18)
    - *“Thank you, please come back soon. I heard there were some undead near an old abandoned house just north of Fallhaven. Maybe you can check…”*


<span id="route-100"></span>

??? note "Stage 100 · Thoronir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Thoronir](../monsters/thoronir.md), choose “I have those bones for you.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30; reached stage 18 of [Score counters (hidden flag)](../quests/scores.md#stage-18); hand over 5× [Bone](../items/bone.md)
    - *“Thank you, these bones will do fine. Now I can start creating some bonemeal healing potions for you.”*


<span id="route-110"></span>

??? note "Stage 110 · Thoronir · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Thoronir](../monsters/thoronir.md), choose “I have those bones for you.”

    - **Needs:** stage 30, 50; reached stage 18 of [Score counters (hidden flag)](../quests/scores.md#stage-18); hand over 5× [Bone](../items/bone.md)
    - *“Thank you, these bones will do fine. Now I can start creating some ... things.”*
