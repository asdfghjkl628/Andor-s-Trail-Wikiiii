    Take care of the caretaker¶
    Quest ID laeroth_caretaker
    In journal Yes
    Stages 21 (completes at 180)
    Started by Moriath (laerothmanor1)
    NPCs involved Audela, Cuned, Jerelin, Jerelin, Moriath, Verigil

    Total XP 18,000
    Related quests 2

    Overview¶

    I met a caretaker at Laeroth manor. He said he cannot leave because he is bound by an oath, but not one he ever gave.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Moriath (laerothmanor1). Required:

    • reached stage 10 of Take care of the caretaker
    • NOT reached stage 30 of Take care of the caretaker
