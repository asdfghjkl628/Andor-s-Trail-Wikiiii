    Overview¶

    Jolnor in Vilegard chapel wants me to talk to Wrye in northern Vilegard. She has apparently lost her son recently.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Jolnor (vilegard_chapel). Required:

    • reached stage 10 of Trusting an outsider

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.

    How each stage is reached¶

    Every dialogue route found in the game data, including alternatives that end up in the same place. "Conditions" are everything checked along that dialogue path. To test a specific situation, open the NPC's page and use its Dialogue simulator.

    Stage 10: 1 route
    1. Talk to Jolnor (vilegard_chapel) → choose “OK. Talk to Kaori. Got it.” — conditions: reached stage 10 of Trusting an outsider → stage 10. NPC: “She recently lost her son in a tragic way. If you can gain her trust, you will have a strong ally here.”
    Stage 20: 1 route
    1. Talk to Wrye (vilegard_wrye) → choose “Can you tell me the story about what happened again?” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause → stage 20. NPC: “I can feel it in me. The Shadow speaks to me. He is dead.”
    Stage 30: 1 route
    1. Talk to Wrye (vilegard_wrye) → choose “What now?” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause → stage 30. NPC: “I fear he has died or got hurt somehow. Those bastards probably drove him into his own death.”
    Stage 40: 1 route
    1. Talk to Wrye (vilegard_wrye) → choose “What can I do to help?” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause → stage 40. NPC: “If you want to help me, please find out what happened to my son, Rincel.”
    Stage 41: 1 route
    1. Talk to Wrye (vilegard_wrye) → choose “Any idea where I should look?” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause → stage 41. NPC: “I guess you could ask in the tavern here in Vilegard, or the Foaming Flask tavern just north of here.”
    Stage 42: 1 route
    1. Talk to Feygard patrol captain (foaming_flask) → choose “OK, that might be something worth checking anyway.” — conditions: reached stage 41 of Uncertain cause → stage 42. NPC: “I noticed he left to the west heading out of the Foaming Flask tavern.”
    Stage 80: 1 route
    1. Talk to Oluag (wild14_clearing) → choose “Maybe he didn't dare tell Wrye?” — conditions: reached stage 80 of Uncertain cause; reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause → stage 80. NPC: “So, anyway. Now he is buried over there. Rest in peace.”
    Stage 90: 1 route
    1. Talk to Wrye (vilegard_wrye) → choose “Yes, but not for long. He did not survive the wounds. He is now buried to the northwest of Vilegard.” — conditions: reached stage 40 of Uncertain cause; reached stage 80 of Uncertain cause → stage 90. NPC: “Oh my poor boy. What have I done?”
