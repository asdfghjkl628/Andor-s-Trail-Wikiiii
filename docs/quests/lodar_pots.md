## Overview

> Lodar says that he can make potions from some animal remains, if I first bring him some Spotted Hornbeam fungus. Apparently, the potion-maker in Fallhaven has a stock of it, if you know enough to ask.

## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Lodar](../monsters/lodar.md) ([Lodarhouse 1](../maps/lodarhouse1.md)). Required:

- reached stage 60 of [Searching for madness](../quests/lodar2.md#stage-60)

??? note "Stage 10 · Lodar · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Lodar](../monsters/lodar.md), choose “Fallhaven? The potion maker in Fallhaven?”

    - **Needs:** reached stage 60 of [Searching for madness](../quests/lodar2.md#stage-60)
    - *“Yes, that's what I was looking for. Fallhaven. Go visit him and ask him if he has some. I am sure he has some, if you know enough to ask.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Potion merchant · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Potion merchant](../monsters/potion_merchant.md), choose “I was told that I can get some Spotted Hornbeam fungus from you.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10
    - **Gives:** [Spotted Hornbeam fungus](../items/hornbeam.md)
    - *“Here, have some. I don't have that much, so don't lose it!”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Lodar · 1 way"

    - **Needs:** stage 10; reached stage 60 of [Searching for madness](../quests/lodar2.md#stage-60); hand over 1× [Spotted Hornbeam fungus](../items/hornbeam.md)
    - *“Oh yes, this will do nicely. Good, good. Thank you, my friend.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

    - **Needs:** stage 30; reached stage 60 of [Searching for madness](../quests/lodar2.md#stage-60)
    - *“With your help, I can now create additional potions from the remains of certain animals if you would like.”*
