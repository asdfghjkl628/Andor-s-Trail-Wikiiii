    Overview¶

    Farrik in the Fallhaven Thieves' Guild told me of a plan to help a fellow thief escape from the Fallhaven jail.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    How each stage is reached¶

    Every dialogue route found in the game data, including alternatives that end up in the same place. "Conditions" are everything checked along that dialogue path. To test a specific situation, open the NPC's page and use its Dialogue simulator.

    Stage 10: 1 route
    1. Talk to Farrik (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “What did he do?” → stage 10. NPC: “I guess I can trust you with this secret. We are planning a mission tonight to help him out of jail.”
    Stage 20: 1 route
    1. Talk to Farrik (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “I am not done yet, but I am working on it.” — conditions: reached stage 20 of Night visit; NOT reached stage 30 of Night visit → stage 20. NPC: “Good. Report back to me when you have gotten the guard captain to drink that special mead.”
    Stage 25: 1 route
    1. Talk to Thieves guild cook (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “Farrik said you can prepare me a round of special mead.” — conditions: reached stage 20 of Night visit → stage 25; also gives Prepared sleepy mead. NPC: “There. This should do it. Here you go.”
    Stage 30: 1 route
    1. Talk to Farrik (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “Maybe I should tell the guards that you are planning to get him out?” — conditions: NOT reached stage 20 of Night visit → stage 30. NPC: “Whatever, they wouldn't believe you anyway.”
    Stage 32: 1 route
    1.     Stage 40: 1 route
    1. Talk to Guard captain (fallhaven_prison) → choose “I heard they are planning his escape tonight.” — conditions: reached stage 30 of Night visit → stage 40. NPC: “Tonight? Thank you for this information. We will make sure to increase the security tonight then, but in such a way…”
    Stage 50: 1 route
    1. Talk to Guard captain (fallhaven_prison) → choose “No, not yet. I'm working on it.” — conditions: reached stage 50 of Night visit → stage 50. NPC: “Good. Report back to me when you have told them.”
    Stage 60: 1 route
    1. Talk to Guard captain (fallhaven_prison) → choose “I have 500 gold right here that you could have.” — conditions: reached stage 32 of Night visit; pay 500 gold → stage 60.     1. Talk to Farrik (fallhaven_derelict2) → choose “It is done.
