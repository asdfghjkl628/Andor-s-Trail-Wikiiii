## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-1"></span>

??? note "Stage 1 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “No. Should I?”

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 1
    - *“Well, you see? I make them. Not to brag, but I am in fact, the best baker in Feygard.”*


<span id="route-2"></span>

??? note "Stage 2 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “You said that you bake them, but what is a "feydelight"?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [A Feygard delicacy](../quests/feygard_delicacy.md#stage-1) is 1
    - *“Child, they are a delicious fig pie enjoyed by most Feygardians as an after dinner delicacy, often reserved for holidays and other…”*


<span id="route-4"></span>

??? note "Stage 4 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “What?”

    - **Needs:** stage 4; not yet stage 7
    - *“Yes. You need to get me some dough, two eggs, butter, honey and five Feygard fig fruits. I also need a bottle of wine. A splash for the…”*


<span id="route-5"></span>

??? note "Stage 5 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “Dough! Where am I supposed to find dough?”

    - **Needs:** stage 4; not yet stage 7
    - *“Well, Brightport is known for its wondeful bakeries. I would start there. Of course, you could get some in Feygard too. But you need to…”*


<span id="route-6"></span>

??? note "Stage 6 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “I've been across a great amount of Dhayavar and I've not seen honey anywhere.”

    - **Needs:** stage 4; not yet stage 7; carry 0× [Honey](../items/honey.md)
    - *“How about trying a beekeeper? Geez, you are not the smartest are you?”*


<span id="route-7"></span>

??? note "Stage 7 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “Here, take them all.”

    - **Needs:** stage 4; not yet stage 7; carry 1× [Honey](../items/honey.md); carry 1× [Butter](../items/butter.md); carry 1× [Wine](../items/guynmart_wine.md); carry 1× [Raw dough](../items/dough.md); carry 5× [Fig](../items/fig_fruit.md); carry 2× [Eggs](../items/eggs.md); hand over 1× [Honey](../items/honey.md); hand over 1× [Butter](../items/butter.md); hand over 1× [Raw dough](../items/dough.md); hand over 5× [Fig](../items/fig_fruit.md); hand over 1× [Wine](../items/guynmart_wine.md); hand over 2× [Eggs](../items/eggs.md)
    - <small>Also: starts timer “feydelight_baking”</small>
    - *“Come back later and it will be ready.”*


<span id="route-8"></span>

??? note "Stage 8 · Philippa · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Philippa](../monsters/village_philippa.md), choose “I am wondering, is my Feydelight ready yet?”

    - **Needs:** latest stage of [A Feygard delicacy](../quests/feygard_delicacy.md#stage-7) is 7; 18 rounds passed since timer “feydelight_baking”
    - **Gives:** 1× [Feydelight](../items/feydelight.md)
    - *“Yes. Please enjoy while it's hot.”*
