    Lost treasures¶
    Quest ID nocmar
    In journal Yes
    Stages 15 (completes at 200)

    NPCs involved Myrelis, Nocmar, Unnmir
    Locations galmore_58

    Version note

    From v0.7.0 to v0.8.17, this quest could not be completed: its final step needed Heartstone, which could not be obtained anywhere. It became completable in v0.8.18.

    Overview¶

    Unnmir told me he used to be an adventurer, and gave me a hint to go see Nocmar. His house is just southwest of the tavern in Fallhaven.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Unnmir. Required:

    • reached stage 100 of Drunken tale
