# Failure Mastery

*For every skill level, reduces the amount of lost experience caused by death by 20 % (percentage of existing exp loss value, not percentage points).*

**Category:** utility · **Max level:** 5 · **Obtained via:** Skill points

## Description

For every skill level, reduces the amount of lost experience caused by death by 20 % (percentage of existing exp loss value, not percentage points). 5 levels will remove all experience loss caused by death.
