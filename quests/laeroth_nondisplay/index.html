    laeroth_nondisplay¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID laeroth_nondisplay
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 32
    Started by stepping on a trigger on island_underground1, stepping on a trigger on island_underground3
    NPCs involved Callista, the centaur, Forenza, Forenza, Gylew, Moriath, Orion, the centaur +1
    Locations island1, island2, island3, laerothbasement2
    Related quests 6
