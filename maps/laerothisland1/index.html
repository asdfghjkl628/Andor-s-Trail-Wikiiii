    Laerothisland 1¶
    Map ID laerothisland1
    Region Near Lake Laeroth (other)

    Size 27×23 tiles
    World map World 1

    Laerothisland 1 is an outdoor map, near Lake Laeroth (other). It has no NPCs and 6 kinds of enemy. Exits lead to Laerothisland 0, Laerothisland 2.
