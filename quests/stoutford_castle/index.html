    Stoutford's old castle¶

    Overview¶

    I tried to free this castle from the undead Lord Erwyn. But whenever I kill him, he reappears. I should seek help...

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on stoutford_castle0. Required:

    • killed 1× Lord Erwyn
    • reached stage 48 of stn_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 49 of stn_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • killed 2× Lord Erwyn
