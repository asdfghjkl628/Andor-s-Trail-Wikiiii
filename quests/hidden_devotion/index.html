    hidden_devotion¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID hidden_devotion
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 24
    Started by stepping on a trigger on undertell_3_02
    NPCs involved Anoa, Forsaken shade, Forsaken shade, Forsaken shade, Forsaken shade, Forsaken shade +6
    Locations undertell_3_00, undertell_3_02, undertell_3_03, undertell_3_10
    Related quests 2

    Overview¶

    PC killed shade1

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on undertell_3_02. Required:

    • NOT reached stage 450 of Devotion
    • NOT reached stage 480 of Devotion
    • NOT reached stage 1 of hidden_devotion (hidden flag)
    • killed 1× Forsaken shade

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
