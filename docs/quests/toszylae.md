## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

    - **Needs:** stage 15
    - *“Regardless, it must be stopped, whatever it means. Maybe it refers to something deeper down this cave? I have not ventured further into…”*


<span id="route-11"></span>

??? note "Stage 11 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), choose “What have you translated so far?”

    - **Needs:** stage 15
    - *“The last part of this piece has been eroded from the rock. It begins with 'Kulauil hamar urum Kazaul'te'. But what is the rest of that?”*


<span id="route-15"></span>

??? note "Stage 15 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), choose “No, I have not found any clues yet.”

    - **Needs:** stage 15
    - *“Also, I should warn you that I believe the shrine talks of a powerful creature somewhere in this cave. Maybe if you find that creature, it…”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Radiant guardian · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Radiant guardian](../monsters/toszylae_guard.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 15
    - *“Kulauil hamar urum Kazaul'te. Kazaul hamat urul.”*


<span id="route-21"></span>

??? note "Stage 21 · Radiant guardian · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Radiant guardian](../monsters/toszylae_guard.md), choose “[Attack]”

    - **Needs:** stage 32
    - *“[As you try to make your attack against the guardian, your arms are held back by an enormous force]”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), choose “Yes, the creature also spoke the words 'Kazaul hamat urul', maybe that is part of the missing piece?”

    - **Needs:** stage 15, 20
    - *“Excellent work my friend! Now I just need to translate it.”*


<span id="route-32"></span>

??? note "Stage 32 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), choose “No, not yet. But I am working on it.”

    - **Needs:** stage 32
    - *“Good. Please return as soon as possible.”*


<span id="route-42"></span>

??? note "Stage 42 · Radiant guardian · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Radiant guardian](../monsters/toszylae_guard.md), choose “Klatam ur turum Kazaul'te”

    - **Needs:** stage 32
    - *“Kulum Kazaul.”*


<span id="route-45"></span>

??? note "Stage 45 · Radiant guardian · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Radiant guardian](../monsters/toszylae_guard.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 45
    - *“[It raises its claw-like hands above its head, looking to get ready to strike at you]”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · Toszylae · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Toszylae](../monsters/toszylae.md), automatic

    - **Gives:** applies condition rotworm, sets stage 10 of [I have it in me](../quests/maggots.md#stage-10)
    - *“[As if having swallowed a thousand needles, you are suddenly stricken with a cascading series of spikes of pain throughout your stomach]”*


<span id="route-60"></span>

??? note "Stage 60 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 60
    - *“Oh, what have I done? I made you say it, and now you are touched by its vile essence.”*


<span id="route-70"></span>

??? note "Stage 70 · Ulirfendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Ulirfendor](../monsters/ulirfendor.md), choose “I at least defeated the lich that infected me with this thing.”

    - **Needs:** stage 60; reached stage 10 of [The dark protector](../quests/darkprotector.md#stage-10)
    - *“Oh, that is good news indeed. A lich you say? With your help, the people of the surrounding towns should be safe from whatever mischief…”*
