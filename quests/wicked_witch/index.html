    Overview¶

    Bela, the Fallhaven tavern keeper heard from a customer about a possible kidnapping of a girl. I should ask around town if anyone knows more.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Bela. Required:

    • reached stage 90 of A giant snake
    • NOT reached stage 50 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 70 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 95 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 96 of A Wicked witch
