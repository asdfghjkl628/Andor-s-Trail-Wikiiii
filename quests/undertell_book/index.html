    Undertell: What was not written¶
    Quest ID undertell_book
    In journal Yes
    Stages 8 (completes at 90)
    Started by stepping on a trigger on Undertell exit
    NPCs involved Arcir, Brenor, Elytharan cooker slave, Ysrine
    Locations Undertell 1 0, Undertell 1 1

    Overview¶

    I found the skeletal remains of an adventurer near the mining rails in Undertell. Among the remains were a history book titled "Undertell: Its Ghosts and History" and a necklace marked "Jewel of Fallhaven", which made me wonder if this man was from Fallhaven.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on Undertell exit. Required:

    • NOT reached stage 10 of Undertell: What was not written

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 10 · stepping on a trigger on undertell_exit · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Undertell exit, choose “Search the remains.”

    • Needs: not yet stage 10
    • Gives: 1× Undertell: Its Ghosts and History, 1× Jewel of Fallhaven
    • “A book is lodged near the ribcage, its oilcloth wrapping stiff and darkened with blood. Despite the damage, the title remains readable:…”

    Stage 30 · Ysrine · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Ysrine, choose “What do you mean?”

    • Needs: stage 40; not yet stage 90; reached stage 10 of The fifth master; not reached stage 7 of Undertell story flags (hidden flag); carry 1× Undertell: Its Ghosts and History
    • “The dead are accustomed to being spoken for. Few think to listen instead. Undertell remembers those who do.”

    Stage 40 · Arcir · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Arcir, choose “What is it leaving out?”

    • Needs: not yet stage 40; carry 1× Undertell: Its Ghosts and History
    • Gives: spawns monsters on undertell_1_1
    • “The Elytharan followers sent into those mines did not vanish. They were made unrecordable. If even one of them still remembers themselves,…”

    Stage 50 · Brenor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Brenor, choose “Who are you?”

    • Needs: stage 40; not carry 1× Heartstone
    • “My name was Brenor. I came from the lands ruled by Feygard, back when its people still followed Elythara. I was taken during the rise of…”

    Stage 60 · Elytharan cooker slave · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Elytharan cooker slave, choose “Who do you cook for?”

    • Needs: stage 40; not yet stage 60
    • “Those who work, eat. Those who eat return to work. The bell decides.”

    Stage 70 · walking into a blocked passage on undertell_1_1 · 1 way

    Way 1: Walking into a blocked passage on Undertell 1 1

    • Needs: not yet stage 70; reached stage 75 of Undertell story flags (hidden flag)
    • Gives: spawns monsters on undertell_01
    • “You say nothing.”

    Stage 80 · Brenor, walking into a blocked passage on undertell_1_1, Ely · 3 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Brenor, choose “I must continue my search.”

    • Needs: stage 50, 60, 70; not yet stage 80; not carry 1× Heartstone

    Way 2: Walking into a blocked passage on Undertell 1 1

    • Needs: stage 50, 60, 70; not yet stage 80

    Way 3: Talk to Elytharan cooker slave, choose “I see, but...”

    • Needs: stage 50, 60, 70; not yet stage 80
    • “When the bell rings, the bowls are filled.”
