    Version note

    As of v0.8.18, this quest cannot be completed: it had no ending yet. This has been the case since v0.7.8.

    Overview¶

    You met Burhczyd afgz Dtaloumiye, a likable young man, in a tavern. He wanted to see the world and followed your advice to travel as a trader from city to city.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    None: talk to Burhczyd (Crossglen hall) to begin.

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
