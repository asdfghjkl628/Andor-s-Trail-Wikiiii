    The odd coin collector¶
    Quest ID odd_coin_collector
    In journal Yes
    Stages 25 (completes at 100, 105, 110, 115)
    Started by Gylew (Waterway 5)
    NPCs involved Forenza, Forenza, Gylew
    Locations Laerothbasement 2, Waterway 5, Waytobrimhaven 3
    Total XP 28,150
    Related quests 5

    Overview¶

    I traded 5 of my 'mermaid coins' to Gylew for 500 gold.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Gylew (Waterway 5). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 100 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 105 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 20 of The odd coin collector
    • reached stage 220 of The silver scale
    • have 5 gold
    • NOT reached stage 10 of The odd coin collector
    • NOT reached stage 11 of The odd coin collector
    • pay 5 gold

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
