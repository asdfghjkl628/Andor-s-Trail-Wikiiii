    Overview¶

    A mage called Vacor in southwest Fallhaven has been trying to cast a rift spell. There was something not right about him, he seemed very obsessed with his spell. Something about him gaining a power from it.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Vacor (Fallhaven south-west). Required:

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 10 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “Could you tell me the whole story again?”

    • Needs: stage 20
    • “Oh, the power I could have had. My dear rift spell.”

    Stage 20 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “What was I supposed to do again?”

    • Needs: stage 20
    • “OK, find the four pieces of my rift spell that the bandits took, and bring the pieces to me.”

    Stage 30 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “I have found all the pieces.”

    • Needs: stage 20; hand over 4× Piece of Vacor's spell
    • “Oh, you found all four pieces? Hurry, give them to me.”

    Stage 40 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “Could you tell me the story again?”

    • Needs: stage 40
    • “I need you to find Unzel and kill him for me. He can probably be found somewhere southwest of Fallhaven.”

    Stage 50 · Unzel · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Unzel, choose “I will listen to your story.”

    • Needs: stage 40, 50
    • “Either you side with Vacor and his rift spell, or side with the Shadow, and help me get rid of him. Who will you help?”

    Stage 51 · Unzel · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Unzel, choose “I will side with you. The Shadow must not be disturbed.”

    • Needs: stage 40, 50
    • “Thank you my friend. We will keep the Shadow safe from Vacor.”

    Stage 53 · Unzel · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Unzel, choose “Hah, I will enjoy killing you!”

    • Needs: stage 40
    • “Very well, let's fight then.”

    Stage 54 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “No. You must be stopped.”

    • Needs: stage 40, 51
    • “Bah, lowly creature. I knew I shouldn't have trusted you. Now you will die along with your precious Shadow.”

    Stage 60 · Vacor · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Vacor, choose “I have dealt with him. Here is his ring.”

    • Needs: stage 40; hand over 1× Unzel's ring
    • “Ha ha, Unzel is dead! That pathetic creature is gone!”

    Stage 61 · Unzel · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Unzel, choose “Yes, I have dealt with him.”

    • Needs: stage 51; hand over 1× Vacor's ring
    • Gives: Gold coins
    • “You killed him? You have my thanks friend. Now we are safe from Vacor's rift spell. Here, take these coins for your help.”
