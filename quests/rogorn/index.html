    Overview¶

    Minarra up in the tower at the Crossroads guardhouse has seen a band of rogues heading west from the guardhouse, towards Carn Tower. Minarra was sure they matched the description of some men whose heads have a bounty on them from the Feygard patrol. If these are the men that Minarra thinks, they are supposedly led by particularly ruthless savage named Rogorn.

    Stage 20 · Minarra · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Minarra, choose “I could go look for them if you want.”

    • Needs: stage 10
    • “They have stolen three pieces of a very valuable painting from Feygard, from the report that I have read. For their crimes and the…”

    Stage 21 · Minarra · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Minarra, choose “I will be back once they are dead. Anything else?”

    • Needs: stage 10
    • “I urge you not to listen to their lies. Their crimes must be punished in order to uphold the law.”

    Stage 30 · Rogorn · 1 way

    • Needs: stage 20
    • “That depends, why do you want to know?”

    Stage 45 · Rogorn · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Rogorn, choose “What now? I was sent here to find you by some guards in the Crossroads guardhouse.”

    Stage 50 · Minarra · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Minarra, choose “Yes, I killed them and recovered the three pieces of the painting.”
