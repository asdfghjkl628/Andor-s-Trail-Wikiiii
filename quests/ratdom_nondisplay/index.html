    ratdom_nondisplay¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track your progress behind the scenes. It never shows up in your journal. The entries below are the developers' notes to themselves, so expect them to be terse.
    Quest ID ratdom_nondisplay
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 88
    Started by stepping on a trigger on home
    NPCs involved Clevred, Fraedro, Librarian, Loirash, Roundling, Wart +2
    Locations blackwater_mountain55, crossglen_cave, home, ratdom_maze1
    Related quests 11

    Overview¶

    1=Ratdom active

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with stepping on a trigger on home. Required:

    • NOT reached stage 1 of ratdom_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 940 of Yellow is it
    • NOT reached stage 942 of Yellow is it
    • NOT reached stage 999 of Yellow is it
    • reached stage 100 of Rat infestation
    • reached stage 30 of Disallowed substance
    • reached stage 60 of Searching for madness
    • reached stage 115 of Destined for great things
    • reached stage 190 of Colonel Lutarc
    • have 9,000 gold

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
