# Dark blessing of the Shadow

*Lowers the chance of being afflicted with all types of conditions by 5 %.*

**Category:** immunity · **Max level:** 1 · **Obtained via:** Quest reward only

## Description

Lowers the chance of being afflicted with all types of conditions by 5 %. This includes all types of conditions caused by monster attacks such as Poison, Dazed or Fatigue.

## Where to learn it

Granted by an NPC as part of: [The dark protector](../quests/darkprotector.md)
