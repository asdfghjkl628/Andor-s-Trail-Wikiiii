    The exploded star¶
    Quest ID mg2_exploded_star
    In journal Yes
    Stages 30 (completes at 20, 40, 46, 48, 50, 52, 60, 62)
    Started by Teccow (Wild 22)
    NPCs involved Kealwea, Pangitain, Teccow
    Locations Brimhaven fortune teller, Sullengard church, Wild 22
    Total XP 157,000
    Related quests 3

    Overview¶

    Teccow wouldn't talk to me at the moment. I should get stronger and come back later.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Teccow (Wild 22). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 10 of The exploded star
    • NOT reached stage 80 of Beer Bootlegging

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
