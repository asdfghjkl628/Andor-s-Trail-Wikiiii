    Priceful vengeance¶
    Quest ID brightport_goons
    In journal Yes
    Stages 17 (completes at 136, 90, 100, 120, 140)
    Started by Barthold (brightport_benbyr)

    Related quests 2

    Overview¶

    Inside a house in Brightport, I met two people named Dynes and Barthold. It turned out that they are the friends Benbyr told me to visit. Rather lacking in hospitality, they offered me work.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Barthold (brightport_benbyr). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 139 of brightport_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • NOT reached stage 130 of Priceful vengeance
    • reached stage 129 of brightport_nondisplay (hidden flag)

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
