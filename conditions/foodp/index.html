    Removal and protection¶

    • Resistance: Enduring Body, −10% of the chance per level (30% → 27% at level 1). 100% chances can't be resisted.
    • Dark blessing of the Shadow −5% of the chance for any condition.
    • Rejuvenation: each round, a 20% chance per round to weaken one timed harmful condition by 1.
    • Removed by Antidote (when used).
    • Removed by Fermented garlic (when used).
    • Duration and rest: timed ones wear off, or rest them away.
