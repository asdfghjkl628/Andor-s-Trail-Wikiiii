## Overview

> Fiamma, the swordsmith of Brightport. Is willing to forge me a sword if I can bring him the materials.

## Prerequisites to start

Start with [Fiamma](../monsters/brightportsmith.md) ([Brightport weapon](../maps/brightport_weapon.md)). Required:

- reached stage 87 of [Brightport story flags (hidden flag)](../quests/brightport_nondisplay.md#stage-87)
- NOT reached stage 20 of [Too hot to handle](../quests/brightport_fiamma.md#stage-20)
