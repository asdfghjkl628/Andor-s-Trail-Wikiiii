## How to reach each stage

Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the **dialogue simulator** on the NPC's page.

<span id="route-10"></span>

??? note "Stage 10 · Tahalendor · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “What?”

    - *“Go away! You're not welcome here!”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “Actually, it's the first time we have met.”

    - *“Nonsense! Go away!”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Yolgen · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Yolgen](../monsters/yolgen.md), choose “That must be my brother Andor!”

    - **Needs:** stage 10
    - *“This church is the refuge for all the villagers when monsters attack. We are really worried now.”*


<span id="route-25"></span>

??? note "Stage 25 · Quiet thief · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Quiet thief](../monsters/stoutford_thief.md), choose “Help me do what?”

    - **Needs:** stage 20; reached stage 70 of [Night visit](../quests/farrik.md#stage-70)
    - *“A kid that looked like you was here. He apparently did some business with the owner and the regulars.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Glasforn · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Glasforn](../monsters/stoutford_innkeeper.md), choose “Why?”

    - **Needs:** stage 20
    - *“Well, you see, I keep some beds for occasions like this one. You can use the one in the corner, near the painting, if you wish to rest.”*


<span id="route-50"></span>

??? note "Stage 50 · stepping on a trigger on stoutford_cellar2 · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Stepping on a trigger on [Stoutford cellar 2](../maps/stoutford_cellar2.md)

    - **Needs:** not yet stage 50; killed 1× [Eliszylae](../monsters/stoutford_lich.md)
    - **Gives:** changes map stoutford_tavern
    - *“You hear a loud rumble, then nothing. Silence. What was that monster?”*


<span id="route-60"></span>

??? note "Stage 60 · Glasforn · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Glasforn](../monsters/stoutford_innkeeper.md), automatic

    - **Needs:** stage 60
    - *“Wait. OK. I stand no chance against you. I'll tell you all I know.”*


<span id="route-70"></span>

??? note "Stage 70 · Glasforn · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Glasforn](../monsters/stoutford_innkeeper.md), choose “I guess you are lucky I visited, although just asking me to kill it would have been easier. And nicer.”

    - **Needs:** stage 60
    - **Gives:** 1× [Necklace of the Undead](../items/necklace_undead.md)
    - *“I'm sorry! How was I to know you could actually kill it? You're just a kid! Anyway, now I can remove the necklace. Please take it. I never…”*


<span id="route-80"></span>

??? note "Stage 80 · Yolgen · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Yolgen](../monsters/yolgen.md), choose “Yes. I think I found the cause.”

    - **Needs:** stage 50
    - *“Great. You should talk to my master. I told him you were not who he thought you were.”*


<span id="route-90"></span>

??? note "Stage 90 · Tahalendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “These? You mean there are others?”

    - **Needs:** stage 80; carry 1× [Demon heart](../items/eliszylae_heart.md)
    - **Gives:** [Green apple](../items/apple_green.md), [Bread](../items/bread.md), [Major potion of health](../items/health_major2.md)
    - *“You know, since those monsters' attacks, we don't have much to offer, but take these. By the way, do you have any idea who might be…”*


<span id="route-100"></span>

??? note "Stage 100 · Tahalendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “Not really.”

    - **Needs:** stage 90
    - *“It's a shame we cannot punish the culprits. Thank you for your help kid.”*


<span id="route-103"></span>

??? note "Stage 103 · Tahalendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “It was all Glasforn's doing. The tavern owner.”

    - **Needs:** stage 70, 90
    - **Gives:** removes monsters from stoutford_tavern
    - *“That fool! We'll make him pay. Thank you for your help kid.”*


<span id="route-106"></span>

??? note "Stage 106 · Tahalendor · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tahalendor](../monsters/tahalendor.md), choose “It was my brother Andor. I need to find him.”

    - **Needs:** stage 70, 90
    - *“That is troublesome. I have no idea where he went when he left Stoutford, but Kazaul has always been linked to the Undertell, south of…”*
