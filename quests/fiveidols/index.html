    The five idols¶
    Quest ID fiveidols
    In journal Yes
    Stages 20 (completes at 70, 100)
    Started by Algangror (lonelyhouse0)
    NPCs involved Algangror
    Locations lonelyhouse0
    Total XP 21,000
    Related quests 3

    Overview¶

    Algangror wants me to help her with a task. She cannot describe the nature of the task, or the reasoning behind it. If I help her, she has promised to give me her enchanted necklace, that apparently is worth a lot.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Algangror (lonelyhouse0). Required:

    • reached stage 21 of Of mice and men
    • reached stage 75 of Everything in order

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
