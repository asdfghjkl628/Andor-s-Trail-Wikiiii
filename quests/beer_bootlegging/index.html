    Beer Bootlegging¶
    Quest ID beer_bootlegging
    In journal Yes
    Stages 12 (completes at 90, 100, 110, 120)
    Started by Feygard patrol captain (Foaming flask)
    NPCs involved Dunla, Farrik, Feygard patrol captain, Kealwea, Mayor Ale, Tharwyn +1
    Locations Fallhaven derelict 2, Fallhaven derelict 2 t, Foaming flask, Sullengard 1 townhall
    Total XP 21,246
    Related quests 8

    Overview¶

    I've agreed to help the Feygard patrol captain investigate why there is so much beer on the Foaming flask tavern property.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Feygard patrol captain (Foaming flask). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 10 of Beer Bootlegging

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 10 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol captain, choose “Anything for the glorious Feygard.”

    • Needs: not yet stage 10
    • “Great to hear. I need you to talk with the tavern owner and find out anything you can about the beer.”

    Stage 20 · Torilo · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Torilo, choose “What does that mean?”

    • Needs: latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 10; pay 10,000 gold
    • “It means that that is all I will tell you and I suggest you talk to another tavern owner.”

    Stage 30 · Tharwyn · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Tharwyn, choose “The thief?”

    • Needs: not yet stage 30; reached stage 30 of Trusting an outsider; reached stage 32 of Sullengard story flags (hidden flag)
    • “That is Dunla. He gets me my beer. Go talk to him.”

    Stage 40 · Dunla · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Dunla, choose “Is the Thieves guild the 'distributors'?”

    • Needs: latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 30; reached stage 50 of The ruthless Crackshot
    • “Yes, yes we are, but if you want to learn more, go talk to Farrik back at our guild house.”

    Stage 50 · Farrik · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Farrik, choose “Yes, it was 'silly' of you to do that.”

    • Needs: reached stage 70 of Night visit; latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 40
    • “It is Sullengard, of course.”

    Stage 60 · Mayor Ale · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Mayor Ale, choose “I guess you could say that.”

    • Needs: latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 50
    • Gives: 1× Mayor Ale's letter
    • “But how can you prove it to me?...Uh, I know. You can deliver this letter for me. Here, take it to Kealwea and return to me afterwards.”

    Stage 70 · Kealwea · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Kealwea, choose “Mayor Ale has asked me to give this letter to you.”

    • Needs: reached stage 91 of Mt. Galmore exploded star (hidden flag); latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 60; hand over 1× Mayor Ale's letter
    • “Oh, I've been expecting this. Thank you.”

    Stage 80 · Mayor Ale · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Mayor Ale, choose “I can't argue with that logic.”

    • Needs: latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 70
    • “Please keep this information to yourself.”

    Way 2: Talk to Mayor Ale, choose “But you are breaking the laws of Feygard. Aren't you afraid you will get caught?”

    • Needs: latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 70
    • “Not as much as we fear not being able to make a living selling our beer. Plus, we have the support of the people of Nor City”

    Stage 90 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol captain, choose “All that work, and the reward is just gold?”

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 80
    • “Anyways, I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”

    Stage 100 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol captain, choose “Thanks and Shadow be with you.”

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 80
    • “I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”

    Stage 110 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol captain, choose “Thanks!”

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 80
    • “I'd like you to know that as soon as I can get word back to Feygard, we will be putting an end to their beer bootlegging operation.”

    Stage 120 · Feygard patrol captain · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Feygard patrol captain, choose “That's the thing, I don't 'know' anything that I could prove.”

    • Needs: stage 10; not yet stage 90, 100, 110, 120; latest stage of Beer Bootlegging is 80
    • Gives: faction “factionCountShadow” set to 4, faction “factionCountThieves” set to 4, faction “factionCountFeygard” set to -4
    • “I knew that you would be a waste of my time. I'll reward you with nothing.”
