    Sutdover story flags¶

    Hidden story flag

    An internal quest the game uses to track progress. It does not appear in the journal. The stage descriptions below are internal notes written by the developers and may be brief.
    Quest ID sutdover_hidden
    In journal No (hidden flag)
    Stages 5
    Started by Emmeline (Lake shore road 1), Bonicksa (Witch house)
    NPCs involved Bonicksa, Emmeline, Rennik
    Locations Lake shore road 1, Wild 6 house, Witch house
    Related quests 3

    Overview¶

    PC has completed enough of 'A Wicked witch' to enable mountain teleportation crystals.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Route 1 (Emmeline (Lake shore road 1)):

    • NOT reached stage 90 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 95 of A Wicked witch
    • NOT reached stage 96 of A Wicked witch

    Route 2 (Bonicksa (Witch house)):

    • latest stage of A Wicked witch is 65

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.

    How to reach each stage¶

    Every route in the game data, including alternatives. To try a specific situation, use the dialogue simulator on the NPC's page.

    Stage 1 · Emmeline, Bonicksa · 2 ways

    Way 1: Talk to Emmeline, choose “What ... do I know you? You're just a young girl?”

    • Needs: not reached stage 90 of A Wicked witch; not reached stage 95 of A Wicked witch; not reached stage 96 of A Wicked witch
    • Gives: sets stage 80 of A Wicked witch, spawns monsters on lake_shore_road_0, changes map lake_shore_road_0
    • “Yes, I was under a spell that made me appear as the witch. She wanted to test your heart. I'm glad you proved kind.”

    Way 2: Talk to Bonicksa, choose “[disgruntled] Is this some kind of sick game to you?”

    • Needs: latest stage of A Wicked witch is 65
    • Gives: sets stage 70 of A Wicked witch, spawns monsters on lake_shore_road_0, changes map lake_shore_road_0
    • “[smirks] Perhaps. But remember, hero, life's full of surprises. You may have won this time, but who's to say what lies ahead?”

    Stage 2 · Rennik · 1 way

    Way 1: Talk to Rennik, automatic

    • Needs: not yet stage 2; not reached stage 57 of Wanted men
    • Gives: Gold coins, Nixite crystal
    • “Ah, $playername has finally returned. Please, take this crystal and some gold as our thanks to you.”

    Stage 3 · stepping on a trigger on lake_shore_road_9 · 1 way

    Way 1: Stepping on a trigger on Lake shore road 9

    • Needs: not yet stage 3; not carry 0× Armored helmet

    Stage 7 · stepping on a trigger on crossglen, stepping on a trigger on · 3 ways
