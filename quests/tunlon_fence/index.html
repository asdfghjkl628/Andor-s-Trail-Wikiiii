    It makes no fence¶
    Quest ID tunlon_fence
    In journal Yes
    Stages 16 (completes at 12, 250)
    Started by Tunlon (bwmfill3)
    NPCs involved Hadracor, Jakrar, Tinlyn, Tunlon, Villager, Wood craftsman
    Locations brimhaven2_woodcutter, bwmfill3, fallhaven_sw, fields6
    Total XP 7,005
    Related quests 5

    Overview¶

    I found Tunlon, a sheep farmer, on the hill next to Crossglen. He asked me to help him get some wood for new fences. Since Crossglen doesn't have a lumberjack, I should go to Fallhaven and ask there.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Tunlon (bwmfill3). Required:

    • reached stage 250 of It makes no fence
    • NOT reached stage 10 of It makes no fence

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
