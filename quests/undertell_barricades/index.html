    Overview¶

    I asked Myrelis how to get the barricades to Undertell removed and he told me to meet his friend Shannal inside the beach-scene house.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Myrelis (galmore_58). Required:

    • reached stage 9 of galmore_nondisplayed (hidden flag)
    • latest stage of Lost treasures is 35
    • NOT reached stage 20 of You shall pass

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
