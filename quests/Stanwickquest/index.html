    No rest for the wicked¶
    Quest ID Stanwickquest
    In journal Yes
    Stages 30 (completes at 110)
    Started by Dibella (brightport_school)
    NPCs involved Bryma, Counterfeit, Dibella, Franz, Frederich, Janwick +4
    Locations brightport1, brightport_forest, brightport_school, brightport_school10
    Total XP 20,000
    Related quests 3

    Overview¶

    I arrived at Brightport's school, only to be told that Stanwick is not allowed visitors.

    Prerequisites to start¶

    Start with Dibella (brightport_school). Required:

    • NOT reached stage 40 of brightport_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • reached stage 40 of brightport_nondisplay (hidden flag)
    • reached stage 110 of Search for Andor
    • NOT reached stage 66 of brightport_nondisplay (hidden flag)

    Verified against v0.8.18 quest and dialogue data.
