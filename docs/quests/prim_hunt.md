    - *“We desperately need help from someone from the outside in our village of Prim.”*


<span id="route-11"></span>

??? note "Stage 11 · Tonis, Prim citizen, Laecca, Guthbered · 4 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Tonis](../monsters/tonis.md), choose “Yes, he told me the story about Prim.”

    - **Needs:** stage 10, 20
    - *“Good, thanks. We really need your help!”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Prim citizen](../monsters/prim_citizen.md), choose “Yes, I am here to help your village.”

    - *“Thank you. We really need your help.”*

    **Way 3:** Talk to [Laecca](../monsters/laecca.md), choose “What beasts are you talking about?”

    - *“Their attacks are getting more and more clever.”*

    **Way 4:** Talk to [Guthbered](../monsters/guthbered.md), choose “Tough luck.”

    - **Needs:** stage 25
    - *“On top of that, there are the attacks from the monsters that we have to deal with.”*


<span id="route-15"></span>

??? note "Stage 15 · Prim citizen, Laecca · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Prim citizen](../monsters/prim_citizen.md), choose “Where can I find him?”

    - *“He is in the main hall right over there. The large stone house.”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Laecca](../monsters/laecca.md), choose “Is there anything I can do to help?”

    - *“You should talk to Guthbered. He is usually in the main hall. Look for a stone house in the center of the village.”*


<span id="route-20"></span>

??? note "Stage 20 · Guthbered · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Guthbered](../monsters/guthbered.md), choose “Any ideas where they might be coming from?”

    - **Needs:** stage 25
    - *“Those evil bastards up in the Blackwater mountain settlement probably summoned them to attack us. They would rather see us perish.”*


<span id="route-25"></span>

??? note "Stage 25 · Guthbered · 2 ways"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Guthbered](../monsters/guthbered.md), choose “What was I supposed to do again?”

    - **Needs:** stage 25
    - *“I want you to go up there to their settlement and ask their battle master, Harlenn, why they are doing this to us.”*

    **Way 2:** Talk to [Guthbered](../monsters/guthbered.md), choose “OK, I will go ask Harlenn in the Blackwater mountain settlement why they are attacking your village.”

    - **Needs:** stage 25
    - *“Thank you friend.”*


<span id="route-30"></span>

??? note "Stage 30 · Harlenn · 1 way"

    **Way 1:** Talk to [Harlenn](../monsters/harlenn.md), choose “Why are you people attacking the village of Prim?”

    - **Needs:** stage 25; reached stage 251 of [The agent and the beast](../quests/bwm_agent.md#stage-251)
    - *“It is, of course, *they* who are the ones causing all the trouble.”*


<span id="route-40"></span>

??? note "Stage 40 · Guthbered · 1 way"
